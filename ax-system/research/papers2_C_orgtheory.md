# C-1. 조직 양손잡이

## 1-1. March (1991) ⭐ 전문 확보

**서지** James G. March, "Exploration and Exploitation in Organizational Learning," *Organization Science* **2(1): 71–87**, Special Issue: Organizational Learning (**1991년 2월**). INFORMS. JSTOR `http://www.jstor.org/stable/2634940`. **[PR]**. 원문 각주: "Accepted by Lee S. Sproull and Michael D. Cohen; received August 18, 1989."

**핵심 주장** 적응 과정은 탐험보다 활용을 더 빨리 정련하므로 단기 효과적·장기 자기파괴적이 된다. 그리고 조직 코드는 오직 그것에서 이탈한 개인에게서만 배울 수 있다.

**초록 (VERBATIM, p.71)**
> "This paper considers the relation between the exploration of new possibilities and the exploitation of old certainties in organizational learning. It examines some complications in allocating resources between the two, particularly those introduced by the distribution of costs and benefits across time and space, and the effects of ecological interaction. Two general situations involving the development and use of knowledge in organizations are modeled. The first is the case of mutual learning between members of an organization and an organizational code. The second is the case of learning and competitive advantage in competition for primacy. **The paper develops an argument that adaptive processes, by refining exploitation more rapidly than exploration, are likely to become effective in the short run but self-destructive in the long run.** The possibility that certain common organizational practices ameliorate that tendency is assessed."

**탐험/활용 정의 — 두 목록 (VERBATIM, p.71)**
> "A central concern of studies of adaptive processes is the relation between the exploration of new possibilities and the exploitation of old certainties (Schumpeter 1934; Holland 1975; Kuran 1988). **Exploration includes things captured by terms such as search, variation, risk taking, experimentation, play, flexibility, discovery, innovation. Exploitation includes such things as refinement, choice, production, efficiency, selection, implementation, execution.** Adaptive systems that engage in exploration to the exclusion of exploitation are likely to find that they suffer the costs of experimentation without gaining many of its benefits. **They exhibit too many undeveloped new ideas and too little distinctive competence.** Conversely, systems that engage in exploitation to the exclusion of exploration are likely to find themselves **trapped in suboptimal stable equilibria.** As a result, maintaining an appropriate balance between exploration and exploitation is a primary factor in system survival and prosperity."

*(탐험은 탐색·변이·위험 감수·실험·놀이·유연성·발견·혁신. 활용은 정련·선택·생산·효율·선별·구현·실행. 활용 없는 탐험 → 미개발 아이디어는 너무 많고 독자 역량은 너무 적음. 탐험 없는 활용 → 차선의 안정 균형에 갇힘.)*

> "Both exploration and exploitation are essential for organizations, but **they compete for scarce resources.** … The explicit choices are found in calculated decisions about alternative investments and competitive strategies. **The implicit choices are buried in many features of organizational forms and customs, for example, in organizational procedures for accumulating and reducing slack, in search rules and practices, in the ways in which targets are set and changed, and in incentive systems.**" (p.71)

*(암묵적 선택은 조직 형태·관습 곳곳에 묻혀 있다 — 슬랙 절차, 탐색 규칙, 목표 설정 방식, 인센티브 체계.)* → **AX 활용:** "탐험을 장려한다"는 선언은 명시적 선택이고, 결과는 예산·평가·승인 절차에 묻힌 암묵적 선택이 결정한다.

**탐험의 구조적 취약성 (VERBATIM, "The Vulnerability of Exploration," p.73)**
> "**Compared to returns from exploitation, returns from exploration are systematically less certain, more remote in time, and organizationally more distant from the locus of action and adaption.** What is good in the long run is not always good in the short run. What is good at a particular historical moment is not always good at another time. What is good for one part of an organization is not always good for another part. What is good for an organization is not always good for a larger social system of which it is a part. … **The certainty, speed, proximity, and clarity of feedback ties exploitation to its consequences more quickly and more precisely than is the case with exploration.**"

> "**Because of these differences, adaptive processes characteristically improve exploitation more rapidly than exploration. These advantages for exploitation cumulate.** Each increase in competence at an activity increases the likelihood of rewards for engaging in that activity, thereby further increasing the competence and the likelihood (Argyris and Schon 1978; David 1985). … **Reason inhibits foolishness; learning and imitation inhibit experimentation.** This is not an accident but is a consequence of the temporal and spatial proximity of the effects of exploitation, as well as their precision and interconnectedness." (p.73)

*(이성은 어리석음을 억제하고, 학습과 모방은 실험을 억제한다. 이것은 우연이 아니다.)*

> "It is quite possible for **competence in an inferior activity to become great enough to exclude superior activities with which an organization has little experience** (Herriott, Levinthal, and March 1985). Since long-run intelligence depends on sustaining a reasonable level of exploration, **these tendencies to increase exploitation and reduce exploration make adaptive processes potentially self-destructive.**" (p.73)

**⭐⭐ 코드는 이탈자에게서만 배운다 (VERBATIM, p.76) — 이 책 논지의 가장 정확한 학술적 대응물**
> "The gains to individuals from adapting rapidly to the code (which is consistently closer to reality than the average individual) are offset by second-order losses stemming from the fact that **the code can learn only from individuals who deviate from it. Slow learning on the part of individuals maintains diversity longer, thereby providing the exploration that allows the knowledge found in the organizational code to improve.**"

*(개인이 코드에 빠르게 적응해 얻는 이득은, **코드가 오직 자신에게서 이탈한 개인에게서만 배울 수 있다**는 사실에서 오는 2차 손실로 상쇄된다. 개인의 느린 학습이 다양성을 더 오래 유지시키고, 그럼으로써 조직 코드의 지식이 개선될 수 있게 하는 탐험을 공급한다.)*

> "…for any average rate of learning from the code, **it is better from the point of view of equilibrium knowledge to have that average reflect a mix of fast and slow learners rather than a homogeneous population. For equivalent average values of the socialization learning parameter (p1), the heterogeneous population consistently produces higher equilibrium knowledge.**" (p.77)

> "On the way to equilibrium, **the knowledge gains from variability are disproportionately due to contributions by slow learners, but they are disproportionately realized (in their own knowledge) by fast learners.** … Prior to reaching equilibrium, individuals with a high value for p1 gain from being in an organization in which there are individuals having a low value for p1, but the converse is not true." (p.77)

*(다양성에서 오는 지식 이득은 불균형하게 느린 학습자의 기여 덕분이지만, 그 이득은 불균형하게 빠른 학습자가 실현한다.)*

> "Since there are no obvious individual incentives for learning slowly in a population in which others are learning rapidly, **it may be difficult to arrive at a fraction of slow learners that is optimal from the point of view of the code if learning rates are voluntarily chosen by individuals.**" (p.77)

→ **"제도가 필요한 이유"의 정확한 미시적 근거.** 개인 유인만으로는 조직이 필요로 하는 이질성이 유지되지 않는다.

**결론부 (VERBATIM, pp.85–86)**
> "**The essence of exploitation is the refinement and extension of existing competences, technologies, and paradigms. Its returns are positive, proximate, and predictable. The essence of exploration is experimentation with new alternatives. Its returns are uncertain, distant, and often negative.**"

> "Such features of the context of adaptation lead to a tendency to substitute exploitation of known alternatives for the exploration of unknown ones, **to increase the reliability of performance rather more than its mean.** This property of adaptive processes is potentially self-destructive."

*(성과의 평균보다 성과의 **신뢰성**을 더 높이는 쪽으로 기운다.)* → AX: 표준화·거버넌스는 산출의 분산을 줄인다. 그런데 혁신의 가치는 오른쪽 꼬리에 있다.

> "**The key factor is the effect of knowledge on the right-hand tail of the performance distribution.**" (p.86)

> Polanyi 인용(원문에 그대로 실림, Polanyi 1963, p.1013): **"I would never have conceived my theory, let alone have made a great effort to verify it, if I had been more familiar with major developments in physics that were taking place. Moreover, my initial ignorance of the powerful, false objections that were raised against my ideas protected those ideas from being nipped in the bud."**

> "…it may be instructive to reconfirm some elements of folk wisdom asserting that **the returns to fast learning are not all positive, that rapid socialization may hurt the socializers even as it helps the socialized, and that the development of knowledge may depend on maintaining an influx of the naive and ignorant**, and that competitive victory does not reliably go to the properly educated." (p.86)

**이 책에 쓸 수 있는 부분**
1. "코드는 이탈자에게서만 배운다"가 논지의 핵심 문장 — 중앙이 현장을 빠르게 표준화·사회화할수록 중앙이 배울 원천 자체가 사라진다.
2. 탐험의 취약성은 도덕적 결함이 아니라 피드백 구조의 결과 → 중앙이 개입하지 않으면 조직은 자동으로 활용으로 수렴한다. 이것이 "중앙이 필요 없다"가 아니라 "중앙이 다른 일을 해야 한다"의 근거.
3. 이질성 유지는 개인 유인으로 안 된다 → 그래서 제도가 필요하다.

**한계·반박** 시뮬레이션 모형 논문이며 실증 데이터가 아니다(두 개의 추상 모형). 파라미터(p1,p2,p3)는 자유 설정이고 현실 학습률에 대응하는 값은 이 논문이 답하지 않는다. "느린 학습자를 두라"는 실무 처방으로 직역하면 위험 — March 본인이 최적 비율 결정을 "nontrivial exercise", "problematic"이라 명시. 이후 30년간 탐험/활용 개념의 과잉 확장·측정 불일치 비판이 축적(Junni 메타분석의 결과 혼재가 그 증거).

## 1-2. Tushman & O'Reilly (1996)

**서지** Michael L. Tushman & Charles A. O'Reilly, "Ambidextrous Organizations: Managing Evolutionary and Revolutionary Change," *California Management Review* **38(4): 8–29**, **1996년 7월**. DOI `10.2307/41165852` (Crossref·OpenAlex 확인). 피인용 4,926회(OpenAlex, 2026-09-05). **[PR]**(실무 지향 저널). **확보 수준: 초록만.**

**초록 (VERBATIM, OpenAlex)**
> "Organizations evolve through periods of incremental or evolutionary change punctuated by discontinuous or revolutionary change. The challenge for managers is to adapt the culture and strategy of their organizations to its current environment, but to do so in a way that does not undermine its ability to adjust to radical changes in that environment. **They must, in other words, create an ambidextrous organization—one capable of simultaneously pursuing both incremental and discontinuous innovation.**"

**이 책에 쓸 수 있는 부분** "양손잡이" 용어의 정전 출처로만 쓰고 실증 근거로는 쓰지 말 것. 이 논문은 **구조적 분리(structural ambidexterity)** 계열의 출발점이고 Gibson & Birkinshaw는 대안인 **맥락적 양손잡이**를 제시한다. 두 계열의 대비 자체가 AX 추진체 설계의 실제 갈림길("별도 조직을 만들 것인가, 같은 조직의 맥락을 바꿀 것인가")이다.

**한계·반박** 실무 지향 저널의 사례 기반 개념 논문. 자체 실증 검정이 없다.

## 1-3. Gibson & Birkinshaw (2004) ⚠️ 초록 수준까지만

**서지** Christina B. Gibson & Julian Birkinshaw, "The Antecedents, Consequences, and Mediating Role of Organizational Ambidexterity," *Academy of Management Journal* **47(2): 209–226**, **2004년 4월**. DOI `10.5465/20159573` (Crossref·OpenAlex 확인). **[PR]** OA 아님(OpenAlex `is_oa: false`, 초록 미제공).

**핵심 주장·확보 수치** 맥락적 양손잡이를 **사업단위 수준에서 정렬(alignment)과 적응성(adaptability)을 동시에 달성하는 역량**으로 정의. **stretch, discipline, support, trust** 조합의 맥락이 맥락적 양손잡이를 촉진하고, 양손잡이가 맥락 특성↔성과 관계를 매개. **표본: 41개 사업단위의 4,195명.**

> ⚠️ **확보 한계.** 위 서술·표본 수치는 AMJ 웹페이지 초록 및 검색엔진 초록 요약 경유이며, **전문·경로계수·유의수준·네 맥락 차원의 원문 정의문은 확보 실패**(AMJ 403, OA 사본 없음). 특히 **"성과와의 관계 수치"는 확인하지 못했다.** 책에 쓸 때 "41개 사업단위 4,195명 표본에서 가설이 지지되었다" 이상으로 서술하지 말 것.

**이 책에 쓸 수 있는 부분 (조건부)** 구조를 쪼개지 않고도 양손잡이가 가능하다는 주장 자체가 논지에 중요 — AX 전담 조직(CoE)을 따로 세우는 구조적 접근 대신 **현장 조직의 맥락(규율·신장·지원·신뢰)을 중앙이 설계**하는 접근. 이것이 "중앙이 공급하는 것은 과업 목록이 아니라 제도"다. 네 차원 중 discipline·stretch = 성과 관리 계열, support·trust = 사회적 맥락 계열로 짝지어 읽으면 거버넌스 체크리스트가 되지만, **원문 정의 미확보이므로 이 해석은 조사자의 것이지 저자들의 것이 아니다.**

**한계·반박** 횡단면 설문 기반이며 성과가 자기보고일 가능성(미확인). 아래 Junni 메타분석이 "지각된 성과"를 쓴 연구에서 효과가 더 강하다고 보고한 것과 겹쳐 읽어야 한다 — 공통방법편의 우려.

## 1-4. Junni, Sarala, Taras & Tarba (2013) ⚠️ 초록 수준까지만

**서지** Paulina Junni, Riikka M. Sarala, Vas Taras & Shlomo Y. Tarba, "Organizational Ambidexterity and Performance: A Meta-Analysis," *Academy of Management Perspectives* **27(4): 299–312**, **2013**(OpenAlex `publication_date: 2013-07-10`). DOI `10.5465/amp.2012.0015`. 피인용 883회(OpenAlex, 2026-09-05). **[PR]** OA 아님.

**초록 (VERBATIM, OpenAlex 역색인 복원)**
> "A growing number of studies argue that organizational ambidexterity is increasingly important for the sustained competitive advantage of firms. **However, organizational ambidexterity studies have been conducted in a wide variety of industries and methodological settings, and the empirical results have been mixed.** The purpose of this article is to systematically examine the organizational ambidexterity–performance relationship to reconcile the mixed results of prior research. By conducting a meta-analysis of prior studies on organizational ambidexterity and performance, we find that **positive and significant Organizational ambidexterity–performance relationships are to a large extent moderated by contextual factors and methodological choices: Organizational ambidexterity is particularly important for performance in nonmanufacturing industries and at higher levels of analysis. Also, the performance effects are stronger when "combined" measures of organizational ambidexterity and perceptual performance are used and when a cross-sectional or multimethod research design is applied.** Our meta-analysis findings advance the field of organizational ambidexterity by revealing some of the moderators in the organizational ambidexterity–performance relationship, thereby providing a more fine-grained understanding of the effects of organizational ambidexterity on firm performance in prior studies. **Our findings point to the gaps and methodological weaknesses in prior organizational ambidexterity research and propose avenues for future research.**"

**이 책에 쓸 수 있는 부분 — 논지의 "정직성 장치"** 양손잡이 문헌을 낙관적으로만 인용하면 안 된다는 경고로 쓰는 것이 가장 정확. 효과는 **비제조업**·**높은 분석 수준**에서 특히 크고, **지각된 성과 측정**·**횡단면 설계**에서 더 강하다 — 뒤의 두 조건은 방법론적 인공물 가능성을 시사. AX엔 오히려 유리(비제조업=지식노동에서 효과 큼)하지만, **"단, 지각 측정·횡단면 설계에서 효과가 부풀려질 수 있다는 것도 같은 논문의 결론"**을 병기해야 정직하다.

**한계·반박** ⚠️ **효과크기(평균 r, 신뢰구간, 이질성 지표, 포함 연구 수)를 확보하지 못했다.** 초록에 수치가 없고 본문 접근 실패. **"메타분석에서 r = ○○"라고 쓰면 안 된다.**

## 2-2. von Hippel (1986) — 리드 유저 정의

**서지 [PR]** Eric von Hippel, "Lead Users: A Source of Novel Product Concepts," *Management Science* **32(7): 791–805**, **1986년 7월**. **DOI `10.1287/mnsc.32.7.791`** (Crossref 확인). 저자 공개 원고 PDF: `https://evhippel.mit.edu/wp-content/uploads/2013/08/lead-users-paper-1986.pdf`
⚠️ 이 PDF의 본문 제목은 **"Lead Users: An *Important* Source of Novel Product Concepts"**로 출판본과 다르다 — **출판 전 원고**다. 아래 문구는 원고 기준이며 출판본과 미세한 편집 차이 가능.

**리드 유저 정의 — 두 특성 (VERBATIM, §3.0)**
> "I define 'lead users' of a novel or enhanced product, process or service as those who display two characteristics with respect to it:
> — **Lead users face needs that will be general in a market place - but face them months or years before the bulk of that marketplace encounters them, and**
> — **Lead users are positioned to benefit significantly by obtaining a solution to those needs.**"

*((1) 시장에서 장차 일반화될 니즈를 마주하되 대다수보다 몇 달 혹은 몇 년 **먼저** 마주한다. (2) 그 니즈의 해법을 얻음으로써 **상당한 이득을 볼 위치에 있다**.)*

**추가 인용 (VERBATIM)**
> Abstract: "Lead users are users whose present strong needs will become general in a marketplace months or years in the future. Since lead users are familiar with conditions which lie in the future for most others, **they can serve as a need-forecasting laboratory for marketing research.** Moreover, since lead users often attempt to fill the need they experience, they can provide new product concept and design data as well."

> §2.0: "**Users steeped in the present are thus unlikely to generate novel product concepts which conflict with the familiar.**"

> §2.0: "These studies thus suggest that typical users of existing products - the type of user-evaluators customarily chosen in market research - **are poorly situated with regard to the difficult problem-solving tasks associated with assessing unfamiliar product and process needs.**"

> §3.0: "**Although the insights of lead users are as constrained to the familiar as those of other users, lead users are familiar with conditions which lie in the future for most - and so are in a position to provide accurate data on needs related to such future conditions.**"

*(리드 유저의 통찰도 다른 사용자와 똑같이 익숙한 것에 갇혀 있다 — 다만 그들에게 익숙한 것이 대다수에게는 미래일 뿐이다.)*

**4단계 프로세스 (VERBATIM, §4.0)**
> "1) Identify an important market or technical trend; 2) Identify lead users who lead that trend in terms of a) experience and b) intensity of need; 3) Analyze lead user need data; 4) Project lead user data onto the general market of interest."

**이 책에 쓸 수 있는 부분**
1. "씨앗은 어디에 있는가"의 좌표 — 모든 바텀업이 동등하지 않고 씨앗은 **두 축(트렌드 선행 × 편익 기대)의 오른쪽 위 사분면**에 몰려 있다.
2. **중앙이 공급할 첫 번째 것은 "트렌드"다** — 4단계의 1단계가 트렌드 식별이고, **리드 유저는 트렌드를 정의하기 전에는 식별조차 불가능**하다. 중앙은 무엇을 하라고 지시하지 않고 **무엇이 선행 축인지를 정의**한다. 그러면 누가 리드 유저인지가 조직 전체에서 계산 가능해진다.
3. 설문 기반 "AX 수요조사"가 왜 실패하는지의 원리적 설명 — 익숙함에 갇힌 다수에게 물으면 익숙한 것의 개선안만 나온다.

**한계·반박** 이론 논문이며 이 1986년 논문 자체에는 리드 유저 정의를 검증한 실증이 없다. "트렌드를 먼저 식별하라"가 순환 논증 위험 — von Hippel 본인 인정: "trend identification and assessment remains something of an art." → **중앙이 축을 잘못 정의하면 하베스팅 시스템 전체가 엉뚱한 곳을 판다.**

1. 가장 큰 선물은 **"커뮤니티 기반 자원"이 4개 모델 모두에서 유의**했다는 것 — 특히 Model 3에서 계수(**1.363**)가 트렌드 선행(**1.370**)과 **거의 동등**. → **리드 유저 특성(누가 그 자리에 있느냐)은 중앙이 못 바꾼다. 그러나 손에 쥔 자원 — 기술 역량과 커뮤니티 접근 — 은 중앙이 공급할 수 있고, 그것이 혁신 발생률과 품질 양쪽을 유의하게 끌어올린다.** "중앙이 공급하는 것은 자원과 제도"의 직접 실증.

**한계·반박** 극한 스포츠 단일 도메인, 자기선택된 취미 공동체 — 기업 조직 외적 타당도를 저자들도 주장하지 않음. 총 응답률 **5.6%**, **91.5% 남성**, 커뮤니티 중복 약 **50%**. **전문가 평가의 코더 간 일치도 Cohen-Kappa = 0.12** — 종속변수 자체가 잡음이 크다. Model 3·4의 **N=79**, "highly attractive"는 **26건**뿐. 횡단면·자기보고 → 인과 주장 불가. 상업적 매력도는 전문가의 **사전 예측**이지 실제 시장 결과가 아니다.

# C-3. 흡수 역량

## 3-1. Cohen & Levinthal (1990) ⭐⭐ 전문 확보 — 최우선 항목 (a)

**서지 [PR]** Wesley M. Cohen (Carnegie Mellon University) & Daniel A. Levinthal (University of Pennsylvania), "Absorptive Capacity: A New Perspective on Learning and Innovation," *Administrative Science Quarterly* **35(1): 128–152**, Special Issue: Technology, Organizations, and Innovation, **1990년 3월**. **DOI `10.2307/2393553`** (Crossref·OpenAlex 확인). 피인용 **35,228회**(OpenAlex, 2026-09-05 조회).

**흡수 역량의 정의 — 초록 첫 문장 VERBATIM (p.128)**
> "In this paper, we argue that **the ability of a firm to recognize the value of new, external information, assimilate it, and apply it to commercial ends is critical to its innovative capabilities. We label this capability a firm's absorptive capacity and suggest that it is largely a function of the firm's level of prior related knowledge.** The discussion focuses first on the cognitive basis for an individual's absorptive capacity including, in particular, prior related knowledge and diversity of background. We then characterize the factors that influence absorptive capacity at the organizational level, how an organization's absorptive capacity differs from that of its individual members, and the role of diversity of expertise within an organization. **We argue that the development of absorptive capacity, and, in turn, innovative performance are history- or path-dependent and argue how lack of investment in an area of expertise early on may foreclose the future development of a technical capability in that area.** We formulate a model of firm investment in research and development (R&D), in which R&D contributes to a firm's absorptive capacity, and test predictions relating a firm's investment in R&D to the knowledge underlying technical change within an industry. …"

*(**새롭고 외부적인 정보의 가치를 인식하고, 그것을 동화하고, 상업적 목적에 적용하는 기업의 능력**이 그 기업의 혁신 역량에 결정적이다. **우리는 이 능력을 기업의 흡수 역량이라 명명하고, 그것이 대체로 기업의 선행 관련 지식 수준의 함수라고 제안한다.**)*

**본문 정의 (VERBATIM, p.128)**
> "We argue that the ability to evaluate and utilize outside knowledge is largely a function of the level of prior related knowledge. At the most elemental level, this prior knowledge includes basic skills or even a shared language but may also include knowledge of the most recent scientific or technological developments in a given field. **Thus, prior related knowledge confers an ability to recognize the value of new information, assimilate it, and apply it to commercial ends. These abilities collectively constitute what we call a firm's 'absorptive capacity.'**"

**조직 수준 — 부분의 합이 아니다 (VERBATIM, p.131)**
> "**A firm's absorptive capacity is not, however, simply the sum of the absorptive capacities of its employees, and it is therefore useful to consider what aspects of absorptive capacity are distinctly organizational. Absorptive capacity refers not only to the acquisition or assimilation of information by an organization but also to the organization's ability to exploit it. Therefore, an organization's absorptive capacity does not simply depend on the organization's direct interface with the external environment. It also depends on transfers of knowledge across and within subunits that may be quite removed from the original point of entry.**"

*(기업의 흡수 역량은 직원들의 흡수 역량의 단순 합이 아니다. … 정보의 획득·동화만이 아니라 **그것을 활용하는 조직의 능력**을 가리킨다. 따라서 외부 환경과의 직접 접점에만 달려 있지 않고, **최초 진입 지점에서 꽤 멀리 떨어져 있을 수도 있는 하위 단위들 사이·안에서의 지식 이전에도 달려 있다.**)*

**⭐⭐ 중앙집권적 게이트키퍼의 실패 (VERBATIM, pp.132–133) — 논지의 핵심 근거**
> "The firm's absorptive capacity depends on the individuals who stand at the interface of either the firm and the external environment or at the interface between subunits within the firm. **That interface function may be diffused across individuals or be quite centralized.** When the expertise of most individuals within the organization differs considerably from that of external actors who can provide useful information, some members of the group are likely to assume relatively centralized **'gatekeeping'** or **'boundary-spanning'** roles (Allen, 1977; Tushman, 1977). …"

> "**A difficulty may emerge under conditions of rapid and uncertain technical change, however, when this interface function is centralized. When information flows are somewhat random and it is not clear where in the firm or subunit a piece of outside knowledge is best applied, a centralized gatekeeper may not provide an effective link to the environment. Under such circumstances, it is best for the organization to expose a fairly broad range of prospective 'receptors' to the environment.** Such an organization would exhibit the organic structure of Burns and Stalker (1961: 6), which is more adaptable **'when problems and requirements for action arise which cannot be broken down and distributed among specialist roles within a clearly defined hierarchy.'**"

*(**그러나 빠르고 불확실한 기술 변화 조건에서는, 이 접점 기능이 중앙집권화되어 있을 때 문제가 생길 수 있다. 정보의 흐름이 다소 무작위적이고 어떤 외부 지식이 기업이나 하위 단위의 어디에 가장 잘 적용될지 불분명할 때, 중앙집권적 게이트키퍼는 환경과의 효과적 연결을 제공하지 못할 수 있다. 그런 상황에서는 조직이 상당히 넓은 범위의 잠재적 '수용기'를 환경에 노출시키는 것이 최선이다.**)*

→ **이 문단이 이 책 논지의 가장 직접적인 학술적 지지.** AX처럼 빠르고 불확실한 변화 국면에서는 **중앙 AX 조직 하나를 게이트키퍼로 세우는 것이 원리적으로 부적합**하다. 답은 "넓은 범위의 수용기 노출" — 현장 다수가 직접 접점을 갖게 하는 것.

**그리고 이것은 "중앙이 없어도 된다"가 아니다 — 바로 다음 문단 (VERBATIM, p.132)**
> "**Even when a gatekeeper is important, his or her individual absorptive capacity does not constitute the absorptive capacity of his or her unit within the firm. The ease or difficulty of the internal communication process and, in turn, the level of organizational absorptive capacity are not only a function of the gatekeeper's capabilities but also of the expertise of those individuals to whom the gatekeeper is transmitting the information. Therefore, relying on a small set of technological gatekeepers may not be sufficient; the group as a whole must have some level of relevant background knowledge, and when knowledge structures are highly differentiated, the requisite level of background may be rather high.**"

*(**소수의 기술 게이트키퍼에 의존하는 것으로는 충분하지 않을 수 있다. 집단 전체가 어느 정도의 관련 배경 지식을 갖고 있어야 하며, 지식 구조가 고도로 분화되어 있을 때는 요구되는 배경 수준이 상당히 높을 수 있다.**)*

→ **AX: 중앙의 일은 게이트키퍼가 되는 것이 아니라, 조직 전체의 배경 지식 수준(=AI 리터러시)을 올리는 것이다.** 그것 없이는 중앙이 아무리 좋은 것을 물어 와도 전달되지 않는다. **"중앙이 공급하는 것은 자원(교육·문서·공용 어휘)"의 근거.**

> "The background knowledge required by the group as a whole for effective communication with the gatekeeper highlights the more general point that **shared knowledge and expertise is essential for communication.**" (pp.132–133)

**이 책에 쓸 수 있는 부분**
1. **"수확"에는 역량이 필요하다는 것을 명명해 준다** — 씨앗이 있어도 조직이 그 가치를 **인식·동화·적용**할 수 없으면 수확은 일어나지 않는다. von Hippel의 "typically rejected as outliers of no interest"가 정확히 흡수 역량의 결핍이다.
2. **경로 의존성이 시급성을 만든다** — "lack of investment in an area of expertise early on may foreclose the future development of a technical capability in that area."
3. **중앙집권적 게이트키퍼 비판이 "탑다운은 지시하지 말라"의 정확한 조건절을 준다** — **빠르고 불확실한 변화**일 때. 안정적 변화에서는 게이트키퍼가 효율적이다.

**한계·반박** 1990년 논문의 실증 부분은 **R&D 지출과 산업 기술 변화의 관계에 대한 계량 모형**이지 "흡수 역량" 구성개념을 직접 측정한 것이 아니다 — 흡수 역량은 R&D 지출로 **대리**된다. 구성개념이 지나치게 넓어 반증 불가능하다는 비판이 축적(Zahra & George 2002의 재개념화, Todorova & Durisin 2007 "Absorptive capacity: Valuing a reconceptualization" AMR 32(3): 774–786, DOI `10.5465/amr.2007.25275513`, Crossref 확인의 재반박). **조직 수준 논의(pp.131–133)는 개념적 논변이며 실증되지 않았다** — "중앙집권적 게이트키퍼가 실패한다"는 저자들의 추론이지 이 논문의 데이터가 보인 것이 아니다. **책에서 "실증적으로 확인되었다"고 쓰면 안 된다.**

## 3-2. Zahra & George (2002) ⚠️ 초록 수준까지만

**서지 [PR]** Shaker A. Zahra & Gerard George, "Absorptive Capacity: A Review, Reconceptualization, and Extension," *Academy of Management Review* **27(2): 185–203**, **2002년 4월**. DOI `10.2307/4134351` (JSTOR 병행) / `10.5465/amr.2002.6587995`. 피인용 7,839회(OpenAlex, 2026-09-05). 선행 판본: *Academy of Management Proceedings* 2000(1): K1–K6, DOI `10.5465/apbpp.2000.5438568` (Crossref 확인). **확보 수준: 초록만.**

**초록 전문 (VERBATIM, OpenAlex 역색인 복원)**
> "Researchers have used the absorptive capacity construct to explain various organizational phenomena. In this article we review the literature to identify key dimensions of absorptive capacity and offer a reconceptualization of this construct. **Building upon the dynamic capabilities view of the firm, we distinguish between a firm's potential and realized capacity.** We then advance a model outlining **the conditions when the firm's potential and realized capacities can differentially influence the creation and sustenance of its competitive advantage.**"

*(기업의 **잠재적(potential)** 역량과 **실현된(realized)** 역량을 구분한다. 그리고 이 둘이 경쟁우위의 창출·유지에 차별적으로 영향을 미치는 조건을 개괄하는 모형을 제시한다.)*

**⭐ 이 책에 쓸 수 있는 부분** **잠재적/실현된 구분이 AX 진단 프레임으로 정확히 쓰인다** — 조직이 외부 지식을 **획득·동화**하는 데까지는 갔지만(잠재적) **변환·활용**으로 가지 못한 상태가 "파일럿은 많은데 확산이 없다"는 AX의 가장 흔한 증상이다. von Hippel의 확산율 표(Table 5.1, 5.0~21.2%)와 겹쳐 읽으면 강하다: **씨앗은 나고(획득), 개별 팀은 쓰고(동화), 그러나 조직 자산이 되지 않는다(변환·활용 실패).** "탑다운의 일은 수확"이라는 명제는 정확히 **잠재적 역량과 실현된 역량 사이의 간극을 메우는 일**이다.

**한계·반박** ⚠️ **개념 논문(AMR은 이론 저널)이며 실증이 없다. 네 차원(acquisition, assimilation, transformation, exploitation)의 정확한 원문 정의문과 "효율 요인(efficiency factor)" 개념은 본문 미확보로 확인하지 못했다. 네 차원 이름을 원문 확인 없이 인용하지 말 것.** Todorova & Durisin(2007)이 이 재개념화를 정면 비판했다(특히 "recognize the value" 단계를 뺀 것과 잠재/실현의 순차성 가정) — 인용 시 이 논쟁의 존재를 밝히는 것이 정직하다.

# C-4. 아이디어 선별 — 누가 좋은 아이디어를 고르는가

## 4-1. Berg (2016) — 창작자 vs 관리자의 예측 정확도 ⭐⭐ 전문 확보

**서지 [PR]** Justin M. Berg (Stanford Graduate School of Business), "Balancing on the Creative Highwire: Forecasting the Success of Novel Ideas in Organizations," *Administrative Science Quarterly* **61(3): 433–468**, **2016**. **DOI `10.1177/0001839216642211`**. 저자 공개 PDF(전문 판독 완료): `https://justinmberg.com/wp-content/uploads/Berg_2016_ASQ.pdf`. 수상: Organization and Management Theory **2017 Best Published Paper Award**.

**초록 전문 (VERBATIM, p.433)**
> "Betting on the most promising new ideas is key to creativity and innovation in organizations, but predicting the success of novel ideas can be difficult. **To select the best ideas, creators and managers must excel at creative forecasting, the skill of predicting the outcomes of new ideas.** Using both a field study of **339 professionals in the circus arts industry** and a lab experiment, I examine the conditions for accurate creative forecasting, focusing on the effect of creators' and managers' roles. In the field study, creators and managers forecasted the success of new circus acts with audiences, and the accuracy of these forecasts was assessed using data from **13,248 audience members**. **Results suggest that creators were more accurate than managers when forecasting about others' novel ideas, but not their own. This advantage over managers was undermined when creators previously had poor ideas that were successful in the marketplace anyway. Results from the lab experiment show that creators' advantage over managers in predicting success may be tied to the emphasis on both divergent thinking (idea generation) and convergent thinking (idea evaluation) in the creator role, while the manager role emphasizes only convergent thinking.** These studies highlight that **creative forecasting is a critical bridge linking creativity and innovation**, shed light on the importance of roles in creative forecasting, and advance theory on why creative success is difficult to sustain over time."

**표본 (VERBATIM 수치)**
> "The participants included a total of **489 individuals: 339 circus professionals and 150 laypeople.** The 339 circus professionals **spanned 43 countries**…" (p.435 부근)
> 일반인 표본: **150명** ("50.0 percent female, mean age = 33.86, S.D. = 10.95")
> 관객: "**A total of 13,248 audience members** from the general public completed the [survey]." 관객 특성: "Audience members had attended an average of **1.96 circus arts shows (S.D. = 2.94)**, and **69.93 percent** had attended at least one circus [show]."
> 비디오: **161개**. 참가자당 평균 **9.62개** 비디오 평가 (S.D. = 0.77), 최소 6개.
> 척도 신뢰도: 관객 즐김 문항 α = **.98**; ICC2 = **.78**, average deviation = **0.64**.

**H1 — 남의 아이디어에 대한 예측: 창작자 > 관리자 (VERBATIM)**
> "For **estimation accuracy** (model 1), the main effect for role was significant, **F (3, 4390.40) = 5.05, p < .01.** Individuals in all roles underestimated the percentage of the audience that would share the videos they rated… Consistent with H1, however, Fisher LSD tests showed that **creators (mean = −3.92) underestimated significantly less than managers (mean = −7.85, p < .001)** but not significantly different from **hybrids (mean = −5.51, p = .31)** or **laypeople (mean = −4.41, p = .64)**. In addition, **laypeople underestimated significantly less than managers (p = .001).**"

> "For **placement accuracy** (model 2), the main effect for role was also significant, **F (3, 3970.22) = 3.44, p < .05.** As predicted, Fisher LSD tests showed that **creators (mean = 7.59) were significantly more accurate than managers (mean = 7.39, p = .01) and laypeople (mean = 7.36, p < .01)** but not significantly different from **hybrids (mean = 7.51, p = .49)**."

**H2 — 위양성/위음성의 방향 (VERBATIM)**
> "…creators … [were significantly less] inclined to commit **false negatives** than **managers (mean = −0.68, p < .05)** and **laypeople (mean = −0.58, p < .05)** but not significantly different from **hybrids (mean = −0.13, p = .60)**. In support of H2, these results suggest that **creators' advantage over managers and laypeople in placement accuracy was partially thanks to creators avoiding false negatives regarding novel ideas.**"

> 각주 8 (VERBATIM): "**When ideas were conventional, all roles tended to commit false positives. Creators (mean = 0.35) were significantly less inclined to commit false positives than managers (mean = 0.83, p < .01)**, but creators and managers did not significantly differ from laypeople (mean = 0.51) or hybrids (mean = 0.32). Taken together, the results suggest that **managers were more likely than creators to incorrectly place conventional ideas above better novel ideas and better average ideas** (ideas closer to the mean in novelty), whereas **laypeople were more likely than creators to incorrectly place average ideas—but not conventional ideas—above better novel ideas.**"

**H3 — 자기 아이디어에 대해서는 창작자가 진다 (VERBATIM)**
> H3a: "…creators … [overestimated] their own videos by **3.46 percent (S.D. = 24.83)**, which was a significant difference, **t(118) = 3.04, p < .01, d = .26.** In terms of placement accuracy, as predicted, **creators placed their own videos significantly too high relative to others' videos (mean = 1.99, S.D. = 3.43), t(118) = 6.33, p < .001, d = 1.16.**"

> H3b: "…in terms of placement accuracy, as predicted in H3b, **creators were significantly less accurate regarding their own videos (mean = 1.67, S.D. = 3.19) than managers were for the same videos (mean = 0.29, S.D. = 2.50), t(63) = 3.86, p < .001, d = .48.** Thus, due to creators' tendency to rank their videos too high compared with others' videos, **managers were significantly more accurate.**"
> (단, estimation accuracy에서는 H3b가 **지지되지 않았다**: 창작자 mean = −2.52, S.D. = 26.99 vs 관리자 mean = −7.61, S.D. = 11.98, **t(63) = 1.45, p = .15.**)

**메커니즘 (VERBATIM, 이론부)**
> "…individuals who are good at divergent thinking (idea generation) tend to be good at convergent thinking (idea evaluation)… **This divergent thinking may help creators stay open minded about how novel ideas may be valuable as they then engage in convergent thinking regarding their own or others' ideas. Thus the very act of divergent thinking may improve creators' convergent thinking by making it more open and flexible.**"
> "…[managers] are likely to engage in convergent thinking without first engaging in divergent thinking and thus **may be more anchored by their domain [knowledge]**… Even for managers who were previously creators, **the benefit of the divergent thinking they did as creators may disappear as they focus on convergent thinking alone.**"

> Study 2 결과 (VERBATIM): "…**creators and manager–creators were both significantly better than creator–managers. Thus divergent thinking led to a boost in accuracy, but this boost disappeared when individuals then focused on convergent thinking.**"

**역설적 함의 (VERBATIM, p.459 부근)**
> "Taken together, these results present a paradoxical tradeoff (George, 2007): **specializing in variation may give creators an advantage over managers in selection, but assigning creators more selection—thus turning them into hybrids—may undermine this advantage. This suggests that role designs may have unintended consequences when it comes to creative forecasting.**"

**⭐⭐ 실무 함의 — 이 절의 결정타 (VERBATIM, pp.461–462)**
> "…creators should be harsher critics of their own ideas… **Managers, however, may benefit from engaging in more idea generation in their jobs, as this may help them avoid false negatives when evaluating novel ideas. Lastly, organizations may want to take steps to leverage the wisdom creators have about their peers' ideas. Instead of having managers be the sole gatekeepers to innovation, organizations could involve creators in selecting which ideas get implemented. Perhaps the best judges of others' ideas in organizations may be those who are busy generating their own.**"

*(**관리자를 혁신의 유일한 게이트키퍼로 두는 대신, 조직은 어떤 아이디어를 구현할지 선별하는 데 창작자를 참여시킬 수 있다. 어쩌면 조직에서 남의 아이디어를 가장 잘 판단하는 사람은, 자기 아이디어를 만드느라 바쁜 사람들일지 모른다.**)*

**⭐⭐ 이 책에 쓸 수 있는 부분**
1. **마지막 문장이 이 책 논지의 가장 아름다운 학술적 대응물.** "탑다운은 지시하지 않는다"의 구체적 형태 중 하나가 **선별 권한의 재배치**다. 중앙이 만드는 "제도"의 하나가 **동료 심사(peer review) 기반 아이디어 선별 기구**라는 설계 처방이 여기서 나온다.
2. **정확한 실패 모드를 준다: 위음성(false negative).** 관리자는 좋은 새 아이디어를 **너무 낮게 놓는다**. 그리고 관습적 아이디어를 좋은 새 아이디어보다 **위에 놓는다**(각주 8). AX 유스케이스 심사에서 정확히 관찰되는 현상이다.
3. **결정적 단서: 창작자에게 선별을 몰아주면 안 된다.** creator→manager 순서(creator–managers)에서는 이점이 **사라졌다**. 즉 **"현장에 다 맡겨라"가 답이 아니라, 창작 중인 사람이 남의 것을 보는 구조**가 답이다.
4. **자기 것은 창작자가 과대평가한다(d = 1.16).** 그래서 **자기 아이디어에 대한 자원 배분 결정은 창작자에게 맡기면 안 된다. 남의 것만 맡겨라.** 이 비대칭이 제도 설계의 핵심이다.

**한계·반박 (저자 본인 서술 VERBATIM)**
- "**both studies focused on ideas that were relatively developed and above a minimum threshold of quality**, but creative forecasting may be different with more-nascent or lower-quality ideas." (단, 저자는 이것이 **보수적** 검정이며 실제 창작자의 이점은 더 클 수 있다고 본다.)
- "**divergent and convergent thinking were not directly measured in Study 1.** Moreover, other differences between creators' and managers' roles may be key in creative forecasting, such as differences in **power** (Sligte, de Dreu, and Nijstad, 2011), **status** (Duguid and Goncalo, 2015), or **accountability pressures** (Tetlock, 1983)."
- "**in both studies, the level of success that ideas could achieve was bounded.** In actuality, some innovations may be exponentially more successful than others."
- **서커스 예술 산업**이라는 단일 도메인이며 성공의 정의가 관객 반응이다. 기업 조직의 아이디어(수익성·전략 적합성·리스크가 얽힌)와는 다르다.
- **효과 크기가 작다** — placement accuracy에서 창작자 7.59 vs 관리자 7.39(10점 척도)는 **0.20점 차이**. 통계적으로 유의하지만 실무적 크기는 크지 않다. **"창작자가 훨씬 낫다"로 과장하면 안 된다.**

## 4-2. Boudreau, Guinan, Lakhani & Riedl (2016) — 평가자는 참신함에 벌점을 준다 ⭐⭐

**서지 [PR]** Kevin J. Boudreau, Eva C. Guinan, Karim R. Lakhani & Christoph Riedl, "Looking Across and Looking Beyond the Knowledge Frontier: Intellectual Distance, Novelty, and Resource Allocation in Science," *Management Science* **62(10): 2765–2783**, **2016**. **DOI `10.1287/mnsc.2015.2285`**. PMID 27746512, **PMC5062254** (무료 전문 — 직접 판독).

**초록 전문 (VERBATIM)**
> "**Selecting among alternative projects is a core management task in all innovating organizations.** In this paper, we focus on the evaluation of frontier scientific research projects. **We argue that the 'intellectual distance' between the knowledge embodied in research proposals and an evaluator's own expertise systematically relates to the evaluations given.** To estimate relationships, we designed and executed a grant proposal process at a leading research university in which we randomized the assignment of evaluators and proposals to generate **2,130 evaluator–proposal pairs**. **We find that evaluators systematically give lower scores to research proposals that are closer to their own areas of expertise and to those that are highly novel.** The patterns are consistent with biases associated with boundedly rational evaluation of new ideas. The patterns are inconsistent with intellectual distance simply contributing 'noise' or being associated with private interests of evaluators. We discuss implications for policy, managerial intervention, and allocation of resources in the ongoing accumulation of scientific knowledge."

**실험 설계 (VERBATIM 수치)**
- 제안서 **150건**, 평가자 **142명**("world-class researchers"), 평가자–제안서 쌍 **2,130개**
- 평가자당 무작위 배정 **15건**
- 기관: 미국 유수 연구중심 의과대학
- 숙주 대학 제출 제안서 **72건/150건**
- 평가자 모집: 6개 그룹에 **180건 초청**(그룹당 30) → **142명 수락**
- 참신함 분석 표본: **689 proposal-evaluator pairs**(숙주 대학 제출분만)
- 평가 점수 SD = **2.58**; 고정효과 제거 후 SD = **1.7**

**참신함 벌점의 성격 (VERBATIM)**
> "**More novel proposals are associated with lower evaluations, with magnitudes of effects comparable to those associated with intellectual distance. It is proposals with particularly high levels of novelty—the 'right tail' of novelty—that account for this result. (For low levels of proposal novelty, evaluation scores were increasing with incrementally greater levels of novelty.)**"

> "**The overall relationship is nonmonotonic, because at low levels of novelty there is an increase of scores with increases in novelty.**"

**⭐⭐ 함의 (VERBATIM)**
> "**Whereas problems with intellectual distance have the potential to 'scramble' rank ordering, problems with novelty have the potential to systematically dissuade experimentation.**"

*(지적 거리 문제는 순위를 **뒤섞을** 가능성이 있지만, 참신함 문제는 **실험을 체계적으로 단념시킬** 가능성이 있다.)*

> "**Any discounting of novel proposals relative to true quality implies underinvestment in novel proposals.**"

> "Unlike, say, the evaluation of prices in markets or product ratings by online 'crowds,' **bounded rationality implies limits to what can be achieved by tallying and aggregating large numbers of opinions.**"

> "**No amount of aggregation and averaging, blinding, or other conventional policies can address this problem.**"

*(**아무리 집계하고 평균 내고 블라인드 처리해도 이 문제는 해결되지 않는다.**)*

**⭐⭐ 이 책에 쓸 수 있는 부분**
1. **"전문가에게 맡기면 된다"에 대한 가장 강력한 반증.** 무작위 배정 실험이다. 그리고 **자기 분야에 가까울수록 더 박하게 준다** — 즉 "그 분야를 제일 잘 아는 사람"에게 심사를 맡기는 상식적 설계가 정확히 **가장 편향된 판정**을 낳는다.
2. **참신함 벌점의 비단조성이 결정적.** 낮은 참신함에서는 점수가 **오르고**, 오른쪽 꼬리에서만 **꺾인다.** 즉 심사 제도는 **적당히 새로운 것을 선호하고 진짜 새로운 것을 벌한다.** Criscuolo et al.(2017)의 역U자와 정확히 같은 형태이며, Benner & Tushman(2002)의 "40%짜리 변주가 먼저 죽는다"와도 짝을 이룬다.
3. **"집계·블라인드로는 못 고친다"는 문장이 제도 설계의 방향을 바꾼다.** 답은 **심사 절차 개선**이 아니라 **심사를 거치지 않는 경로(=white space, 승인 없는 실험 구역)를 따로 만드는 것**이다.

**한계·반박** 과학 연구 제안서라는 특수 맥락 — 기업 내 아이디어 심사와 인센티브 구조가 다르다. **참신함 분석 표본이 689쌍**(전체 2,130쌍이 아님). 참신함은 연구진이 구성한 측정치이며 "진짜 품질"에 대한 독립적 기준이 없다 — 저자들도 "Any discounting of novel proposals **relative to true quality**"라고 조건절을 단다. **이 논문은 편향의 패턴을 보였지 오류를 직접 증명하지 않았다.** 단일 기관 단일 분야(의생명).

## 4-3. Criscuolo, Dahlander, Grohsjean & Salter (2017) — 심사 패널의 참신함 역U자 ⭐ 전문 확보

**서지 [PR]** Paola Criscuolo, Linus Dahlander, Thorsten Grohsjean & Ammon Salter, "Evaluating Novelty: The Role of Panels in the Selection of R&D Projects," *Academy of Management Journal* **60(2): 433–460**, **2017**(OpenAlex online **2016-03-08**). **DOI `10.5465/amj.2014.0861`**. **OA (CC BY-NC-ND)**: `https://purehost.bath.ac.uk/ws/files/158690690/Evaluatingnoveltypdf.pdf` — 다운로드·전문 판독 완료.

**초록 (VERBATIM)**
> "Building on a unique, multi-source, and multi-method study of R&D projects in a leading professional service firm, we develop the argument that **organizations are more likely to fund projects with intermediate levels of novelty. That is, some project novelty increases the share of requested funds received, [but too much novelty is difficult to appreciate and is selected against].** … **We theorize that a high panel workload reduces panel preference for novelty in selection, whereas a diversity of panel expertise and a shared location between panel and applicant increase [preference for novelty].**"

**연구 맥락·표본 (VERBATIM)**
- 대형 다국적 엔지니어링 컨설팅 기업(전문서비스 기업)의 R&D 프로젝트 관리 시스템
- **"The data include all 556 R&D project applications submitted to the system between 2006 and 2008."** — **성공·실패·부분 자금 지원 프로젝트를 모두 포함**: "this source includes successful, unsuccessful, and partially funded projects—data that have previously proven difficult to obtain."
- **패널 8개**, 각자 연간 예산 보유
- 이 기업이 자기 R&D 방식을 부르는 이름: **'bottom-up' approach to R&D** (원문 표현)
- 투명성: "comments, panel members and their decisions, are displayed on the organization's intranet."

**H1 — 역U자 (VERBATIM)**
> "…squared term is negative and significant (**β = −4.31, p<0.01**). **The curve reaches its maximum at a project novelty value of 0.256. The number of observations beyond this inflection point amounts to 39%.** Following the suggestion of Haans et al. (2015), we further confirm the presence of an inverted U-shaped relationship… **When project novelty is equal to its 25th percentile (0.14), the slope of the curve is positive (1.00) and significant (χ²(1)=3.37, p<0.10). When project novelty is equal to its 90th percentile (0.38), the slope is negative (−1.07) and also significant (χ²(1)=12.45, p<0.01).** As an additional test, we derive Fieller's (1954) confidence interval around the maximum of the curve… **The extremes of the confidence interval are 0.133 and 0.372, well within the range of our main independent variable.** … **These results strongly support Hypothesis 1, which predicts that selectors allocate more funds to projects with intermediate levels of novelty.**"

→ **관측치의 39%가 변곡점 너머에 있다.** 즉 제출된 프로젝트의 약 5분의 2가 "너무 참신해서 벌점을 받는" 구간에 있다.

**H2 — 패널 업무부하 (VERBATIM)**
> "The coefficient of the interaction term between project novelty and panel workload in Model 7 is **positive and significant (β=2.33, p<0.01)**, while that of the interaction term between panel workload and project novelty squared is **negative and significant (β=−4.41, p<0.01)**. … **In line with our second hypothesis, an increase in panel workload reduces the panel's preference for novelty. Specifically, the threshold at which negative returns for project novelty occur decreases, meaning that the maximum of the curve shifts to the left, and the curve becomes steeper to the right of this maximum.** … the shift … is statistically significant at the 95% significance level when selectors' workloads are equal to one standard deviation above the mean (**z=−.160, p<0.01**)."

→ **바쁜 심사자는 참신함을 더 빨리 벌한다.**

**H3 — 패널 전문성 다양성 (VERBATIM)**
> "…negative and significant (**β=−2.76, p<0.05**), while the interaction term with the squared term of project novelty is **positive and significant (β=6.21, p<0.05)**. … **as the level of expertise diversity increases, the project novelty curve becomes flatter: that is, the slopes to the right of the turning point become less negative.** We also find that when the level of expertise diversity is one standard deviation below the mean, **the curve's maximum shifts to the left (z=3.89, p-value<0.01).** … **Thus, overall, we find strong support for our third hypothesis.**"

→ **전문성이 다양한 패널일수록 참신함에 대한 벌점이 완만해진다.**

**⭐⭐ 이 책에 쓸 수 있는 부분**
1. **이 논문은 "수확 시스템"을 실제로 운영하는 기업의 데이터다.** 인트라넷에 모든 제안·코멘트·심사 결과가 공개되고, 회사 스스로 이를 "bottom-up R&D"라 부른다. **즉 이 책이 제안하는 제도가 실제로 존재하고, 그 제도 안에서도 참신함 벌점이 발생한다.** 논지를 약화시키는 게 아니라 **정밀화**한다 — 수확 시스템을 만드는 것만으로는 부족하고, 그 시스템의 **심사 부하와 심사자 구성**까지 설계해야 한다.
2. **두 개의 조작 가능한 레버:** (a) **패널 업무부하를 낮춰라**(β=2.33 / −4.41) — AX 유스케이스 심사위원회가 분기마다 200건을 몰아서 보면 그 구조 자체가 참신함을 벌한다. (b) **패널의 전문성 다양성을 높여라**(β=−2.76 / 6.21) — Boudreau et al.(2016)의 "지적 거리"와 정확히 수렴.
3. **Boudreau et al.과의 수렴이 논거를 강하게 만든다.** 한쪽은 무작위 배정 실험(과학 제안서), 다른 쪽은 기업 필드 데이터(556건 R&D 신청) — **서로 다른 방법·맥락에서 같은 형태의 결론.**

**한계·반박** 단일 기업 데이터(엔지니어링 컨설팅, 프로젝트 규모 작음). 참신함은 텍스트 기반 구성 측정치이고 종속변수는 **요청 자금 대비 배정 비율**이지 실제 성과가 아니다 — "참신한 프로젝트가 실제로 더 좋았는데 덜 받았다"를 이 데이터는 보이지 못한다. ⚠️ **회귀표의 관측치 수(N)와 H4(shared location) 결과는 확인하지 못했다.** 2006–2008년 데이터.

## 4-4. Mollick & Nanda (2016) — 군중 vs 전문가 ⭐ 워킹페이퍼 전문 확보

**서지 [PR]** Ethan Mollick (Wharton) & Ramana Nanda (Harvard Business School), "Wisdom or Madness? Comparing Crowds with Expert Evaluation in Funding the Arts," *Management Science* **62(6): 1533–1553**, **2016년 6월**. **DOI `10.1287/mnsc.2015.2207`**.
> ⚠️ **아래 인용은 OA 워킹페이퍼 판본 기준:** HBS **Working Paper 14-116**, 표지 일자 **August 26, 2015**, 본문 일자 **March 5, 2015**. `http://www.hbs.edu/faculty/pages/download.aspx?name=14-116.pdf` — 다운로드·전문 판독 완료. **[WP]** / 게재본 **[PR]**. 문구가 게재본과 다를 수 있다.

**초록 전문 (VERBATIM, WP 판본)**
> "In fields as diverse as technology entrepreneurship and the arts, **crowds of interested stakeholders are increasingly responsible for deciding which innovations to fund, a privilege that was previously reserved for a few experts**, such as venture capitalists and grant-making bodies. Little is known about the degree to which the crowd differs from experts in judging which ideas to fund, and, indeed, whether the crowd is even rational in making funding decisions. Drawing on a panel of national experts and comprehensive data from the largest crowdfunding site, we examine funding decisions for proposed theater projects, a category where expert and crowd preferences might be expected to differ greatly. **We instead find significant agreement between the funding decisions of crowds and experts. Where crowds and experts disagree, it is far more likely to be a case where the crowd is willing to fund projects that experts may not. Examining the outcomes of these projects, we find no quantitative or qualitative differences between projects funded by the crowd alone, and those that were selected by both the crowd and experts.** Our findings suggest that **crowdfunding can play an important role in complementing expert decisions, particularly in sectors where the crowds are end users, by allowing projects the option to receive multiple evaluations and thereby lowering the incidence of 'false negatives.'**"

**설계·표본 (VERBATIM 수치)**
- 전문가: **"we draw on the experience of 30 judges who have deep and extensive knowledge"** — 미국 National Endowment for the Arts 등 기관의 연극 심사 경험자
- 대상: 2009년 5월–2012년 6월 Kickstarter 연극 프로젝트 중 조건 충족 **257건**에서 **무작위 120건** 선정
- 층화: **"our stratified random sample consisted of 60 failed projects (from a total of 141 failed projects), 40 projects that just met their goal (from a total of 85 such projects) and 20 projects that exceeded their goal (from a total of 31 such projects)"**
- 세트 구조: **20 "sets" of 6 projects each** (실패 3 + 달성 2 + 초과 1)
- 평가: 10개 세트는 심사자 2명, 10개 세트는 1명 → **"we have a total of 180 evaluations for the 120 projects"**
- 인센티브: **$50 gift certificate**
- 후속 설문 응답률: **32 of the 60 projects**, 그중 **28건**이 전체 데이터 완비

**핵심 결과 (VERBATIM)**
> "We find … [strong positive correlations] between the realized funding decisions by crowds and the evaluation of those same projects by experts. **Projects that were funded by the crowds received consistently higher scores from experts, were more [likely to receive] funding from the experts. This pattern was equally true for projects that were 'overfunded' on Kickstarter** – that is, projects that raised more money that was asked for. Given the strong positive correlations we observe in crowd and expert evaluations, even in a category with a subjective nature such as theater, this suggests both broad similarities between how experts and crowds judge projects and that **crowds are generally consistent in their choices of which projects to back, allowing us to reject** [the 'madness of crowds' hypothesis]."

> "**Of the projects where there is no agreement, the crowd is much more likely to have funded a project that the judge did not like than the reverse. Around 75% of the projects where there is a disagreement are ones where the crowd funded a project but the expert would not have funded it.**"

> "**We also see a clear pattern in terms of the characteristics of projects that are liked by the crowds: they offer multiple tiers of rewards and provide more updates. In fact, projects that were ranked highly by the experts but not funded by crowds had systematically fewer reward levels**… [suggesting a distinct skill in raising money from] crowds, one that may be systematically different from that of raising money from experts."

> "**Despite the fact that these were not rated as highly by the experts, we find no qualitative or quantitative differences in the long-term outcomes of these projects. They are equally** [successful]…"

> "…lower the incidence of '**false negatives**,' **(that is, viable projects that are turned down by funders) by allowing projects the option to receive multiple evaluations and reach out to receptive communities that may not otherwise be represented by experts.**"

**⭐⭐ 이 책에 쓸 수 있는 부분**
1. **이것이 C-4 클러스터의 균형추다.** Boudreau et al.과 Criscuolo et al.이 "전문가 심사는 참신함을 벌한다"를 보였다면, Mollick & Nanda는 **"군중이 미쳤다"는 반대 우려를 기각**한다. 군중과 전문가는 **대체로 일치**한다.
2. **핵심은 불일치의 비대칭:** 불일치의 약 **75%**가 "군중은 펀딩했으나 전문가는 안 했을" 경우고, **그 프로젝트들의 장기 성과는 전문가도 인정한 프로젝트와 차이가 없었다.** → **군중을 추가하는 것의 가치는 "더 잘 고르는 것"이 아니라 "위음성을 줄이는 것"이다.**
3. **AX 번역(매우 구체적):** 중앙 심사위원회를 **없애라는 게 아니다.** 위원회는 대체로 옳다. 문제는 위원회가 **혼자 유일한 관문**일 때 걸러지는 것들이다. 제도 설계는 **"위원회를 고치기"가 아니라 "두 번째 경로를 만들기"**여야 한다 — 사내 크라우드 투표, 자율 예산, 승인 없는 실험 한도. **"multiple evaluations"라는 원문 표현이 그대로 설계 원칙이다.**
4. **단, 경고도 정직하게:** 군중이 좋아한 프로젝트의 특징은 **보상 등급이 많고 업데이트를 자주 하는 것**이었다. **군중 채널은 "홍보를 잘하는 능력"이라는 별도 기술을 요구한다** — 사내 아이디어 공모에서 "말 잘하는 팀이 이긴다"는 문제가 여기서 예측된다.

**한계·반박** **연극 크라우드펀딩이라는 매우 특수한 맥락** — 저자들도 "군중이 최종 사용자인 부문(sectors where the crowds are end users)"이라는 조건절을 단다. AX 사내 도구는 종종 이 조건을 만족하지만, 전략적·규제적 판단이 필요한 영역은 만족하지 않는다. **심사자 30명, 프로젝트 120건, 평가 180건** — 작다. 10개 프로젝트는 심사자 1명뿐. **장기 성과 비교의 표본이 매우 작다** — 후속 설문 응답 28건, 전문가만 선택한 프로젝트 11건, 군중만 자금 지원한 프로젝트 21건. **"차이가 없다"는 것은 검정력 부족으로 인한 귀무가설 기각 실패일 수 있다** — 반드시 병기할 것. 인용은 **워킹페이퍼 판본**이며 게재본과 대조하지 못했다.

## 5-1. Adler & Borys (1996) — 네 특성 원문 (OCR 판독) ⭐⭐

**서지 [PR]** Paul S. Adler & Bryan Borys, "Two Types of Bureaucracy: Enabling and Coercive," *Administrative Science Quarterly* **41(1): 61–89**, **1996년 3월**. **DOI `10.2307/2393986`** (Crossref·OpenAlex 확인). 피인용 2,183회(OpenAlex, 2026-09-05). OA 아님.

**초록 (VERBATIM, OpenAlex)**
> "This article proposes a conceptualization of workflow formalization that helps reconcile the contrasting assessments of bureaucracy as alienating to employees or as enabling them to perform their tasks better. Interpreting formalization as an organizational technology, we use recent research on the design of equipment technology to identify **two types of formalization—enabling and coercive**. **Whether the impact of formalization on employees' attitudes is positive or negative is, we argue, a function of whether that formalization enables employees better to master their tasks or functions as a means by which management attempts to coerce employees' effort and compliance.** We identify some forces that tend to discourage the enabling orientation to the benefit of the coercive orientation, as well as some persistent countertendencies that encourage the enabling orientation."

**⭐ 문제 설정 — 양이 아니라 종류 (VERBATIM, OCR, p.62 인근)**
> "We argue that this divergence reflects the fact that **while research to date has focused on the impact of different *degrees* of formalization, it has paid insufficient attention to different *types* of formalization.** If we interpret formalization as an organizational technology, we can draw inspiration from recent research on the design of equipment technology to differentiate two generic types of formalization—**formalization designed to enable employees to master their tasks, and formalization designed to coerce effort and compliance from employees**. The attitudinal outcomes are likely very different."

*(지금까지의 연구는 형식화의 **정도**가 미치는 영향에 집중했고, 형식화의 **유형** 차이에는 충분히 주목하지 않았다.)*

→ **이 한 문장이 이 책 논지의 정밀화 자체다.** AX 리더가 흔히 벌이는 논쟁("가이드라인을 더 줄까 덜 줄까")이 **잘못된 축 위에서** 벌어지고 있음을 지적하는 데 쓸 수 있다.

**네 가지 설계 특성 — 총괄 (VERBATIM, OCR, p.70)**
> "Research into equipment design reveals **four generic features** that distinguish deskilling from usability approaches: **repair, internal transparency, global transparency, and flexibility.** Each has strong parallels in the domain of procedure design. … **the common underlying thread is the need to help users form a mental model of the system they are using. If systems break down or if users make mistakes, such mental models enable users to regain control; if the system can be improved, they enable users to formulate and evaluate suggestions for improvement. In the deskilling approach, these mental models are superfluous, because employees are expected merely to follow explicit instructions.**"

**① Repair (수리 가능성) — pp.70–72 (VERBATIM, OCR)**
> "One characteristic of highly usable systems is thus **the ease with which users can repair the process themselves rather than allowing the breakdown to force the work process to a halt.**"

> "The enabling logic, in contrast, generates procedures that facilitate responses to real work contingencies. **Breakdowns and repairs signal to the organization problems with the formal procedures and become opportunities for improvement.**"

> "Toyota's 'standardized work' process provides a salient contrast with traditional industrial engineering practice. **Toyota's focus is not on standards (allotted times) but on methods.** As used at the NUMMI plant in Fremont, California, for example, the standardized work process **brings workers and supervisors together to define cooperatively and to document in great detail the most effective work methods and task allocations. Workers time themselves and conduct their own analysis of the real work process.** … **Deviations from the detailed, prescribed methods signal either the need for further worker training or the need to revise the inadequate standardized work methods.** In this context, the TQM dictum **'You can't improve a process that hasn't been standardized' becomes a philosophy of collaborative learning** (Adler, 1993)."

**⭐⭐ ② Internal transparency (내부 투명성) — p.72 (VERBATIM, OCR)**
> "Procedures designed in a coercion logic are formulated as **lists of flat assertions of duties. They are not designed to help employees so much as their supervisors. They do not seek to guide the employee's efforts so much as sanction punishment in the case of deviations.** The ISO 9000 procedures in the organization cited above **did not provide operators with the rationale for the work procedures.** The worker was expected merely to implement the work instructions. **Their rationale was the province of the engineer.**"

> "In contrast, **enabling procedures provide users with visibility into the processes they regulate** by explicating its key components and by codifying best-practice routines. **They provide users with an understanding of the underlying theory of this process by clarifying the rationale of the rules.** And they provide users with feedback on their performance by providing metrics that help users assess their performance against historical standards."

> "In a product development procedure that specifies a set of documents required at each phase, an enabling-type manual can provide copies not only of the blank forms for these documents but also **the 'best example to date' from the organization's prior projects. In this scenario, the manual becomes a working tool, a resource that is open permanently on the engineer's desk, rather than a set of hurdles that are circumvented covertly.** In one organization we studied, there is a friendly rivalry among design teams, **each vying for the honor of getting their team's documents into the next revision of the manual as best-to-date templates.**"

→ **AX 프롬프트 라이브러리·템플릿 저장소의 설계 사양이 여기 그대로 있다.** 빈 양식만 주면 강압형, **"현재까지의 최고 사례"를 함께 주면 활성화형.** 그리고 **팀들이 자기 산출물을 다음 개정판에 넣으려고 경쟁한다**는 대목은 "bottom-up 씨앗을 중앙이 수확하는 제도"의 완벽한 실물 사례다.

**⭐⭐ ③ Global transparency (전역 투명성) — p.73 (VERBATIM, OCR) — 이 책 논지의 직접 설계 명세**
> "**Internal transparency refers to internal functioning of the equipment or procedure as used by employees; global transparency refers to the intelligibility for employees of the broader system within which they are working.** The global transparency valued in a coercion logic is decidedly asymmetrical, as exemplified by Bentham's Panopticon…"

> "In a coercive approach to procedure design, **global transparency for subordinates is a risk to be minimized.** Tasks are partitioned, and if employees 'move beyond their specific realms' they are told, **'That's not your job'** (Heckscher, 1994: 20)."

> "Some suggestion systems exemplify this approach. In some organizations, **suggestion systems are designed primarily to identify a small number of high-payoff proposals. Relying on the motivating power of large rewards for a very small proportion of submitted suggestions, management is concerned with retaining full control over their acceptance or rejection. The suggestion procedure itself is thus left largely opaque to employees. An employee who submits a suggestion has no clear idea who will evaluate it, according to what criteria, where in the evaluation cycle the suggestion is on a given date, or why it was ultimately approved or rejected.**"

> "In an enabling approach… **The suggestion system at NUMMI, for example, is seen as part of a broad program of employee skill development and employee involvement. It is therefore designed to encourage a large number of suggestions, including many with only very small payoffs.** As a result, the suggestion procedure is explained in considerable detail to all employees. Explanations of evaluation criteria are seen as opportunities to broaden employees' understanding of the production system. **An understanding of the review process and the ability to identify the status of a given suggestion under review are considered essential to motivating continued participation.**"

→ **"위의 일은 지시가 아니라 수확 시스템을 짓는 것"에 대한 가장 직접적인 설계 명세.** 나쁜 수확 시스템 = 소수 대박만 고르고 심사 과정이 불투명. 좋은 수확 시스템 = **소액 성과의 제안도 대량으로 장려하고, 평가 기준과 진행 상태를 제안자가 조회할 수 있게 한다.** Berg(2016)의 "위음성" 문제와 Criscuolo(2017)의 "패널 부하" 문제에 대한 동시 처방이기도 하다.

**⭐⭐ ④ Flexibility (유연성) — p.74 (VERBATIM, OCR)**
> "**The coercive procedure manual defines in detail the specific sequence of steps to be followed in the product-development process and forces the employee to ask for the superior's approval for skipping steps unnecessary for the specific project at hand. The assumption is that the manual prescribes, the employee implements, and only the supervisor can authorize a deviation.**"

> "**An enabling procedure manual assumes that deviations are not only risks but also learning opportunities.** In one organization we studied, the engineering change process… was redesigned along these lines (Borys, 1992). A task-force charged with designing a new procedure found that **under time pressure, engineers sometimes covertly skipped certain steps in the existing procedure. Closer analysis revealed that in some cases these steps could indeed be safely skipped. So the new procedure specified four distinct processes with guidelines on how to choose the appropriate one, with the result that the engineers could now take short-cuts without resorting to work-arounds.**"

→ **워크어라운드를 "위반"이 아니라 "설계 입력"으로 읽는 방법론의 원전.**

**⭐ 강압형이 낳는 것 (VERBATIM, OCR, p.71)**
> "In one organization we studied, **proud of its recent certification as conforming to the ISO 9000 international quality assurance standard, engineers write procedures, then hand them to employees, who must sign them as if they constituted a contract. The rationale offered by management is that this ensures an audit trail in case of quality problems.** From the employees' point of view, the arrangement is experienced as **'a way for the higher-ups to cover their asses.'** The procedure is neither designed nor implemented as an aid for the user; in the words of one worker: **'Sure the engineers discuss the procedure with us. But it's not like we get to discuss it with them.'** As a result, **covert and inefficient 'work-arounds' abound.**"

**⭐⭐ 표준을 누가 만드는가 (VERBATIM, OCR, p.75)**
> "If, as in the scenario hypothesized by Mintzberg (1979), **staff analysts formulate procedures in distrustful isolation from line employees, it is not surprising that those employees resist the resulting system.** At NUMMI, **workers develop standardized work procedures themselves.**"

> NUMMI 작업자 인용 (Adler, 1993: 145 재인용): "**The GM system [of job design] relied on authority. People with rank—the managers—ruled regardless of their competence or the validity of what they were saying…. At NUMMI, rank doesn't mean a damn thing—standardized work means that we all work out the objectively best way to do the job, and everyone does it that way. I might make some minor adjustments because of my height, for example, but I follow the procedure we've laid out because it makes sense. … Management has delegated responsibility to the people who do the work and that gives workers a sense of pride in their jobs.**"

→ **같은 공장, 같은 사람들, 같은 정도의 표준화인데 GM 방식은 소외를, NUMMI 방식은 자부심을 낳았다. 표준의 양이 아니라 저작권(authorship)이 갈랐다.**

**설계 프로세스 요건 (VERBATIM, OCR, pp.74–75)**
> "The literature on the usability assurance process suggests **four key process imperatives** well illustrated in the Xerox case: **an early and continual focus on users, an integrated view of the various aspects of usability, early and continual user testing, and an iterative design process that allows for progressive improvement** (Gould, 1988)."

> "Depending on the relevance of the procedures to the employee, and assuming that the employees are given the appropriate training and resources, **employee involvement in the formulation of procedures is likely to have a positive effect on both attitudinal and technical outcomes.**"

**2차 문헌 경유 인용문 (페이지 표기 포함 — 전부 2차 출처 명시)**
> "**written rules, procedures, and instructions**" (A&B 1996, p.62) — 형식화의 정의. 회수 출처: Scheller & Harrer (2020), "The Paradox of Agility…", `10.5771/0042-059X-2020-2-122` (OA).
> "**four generic features that distinguish deskilling from usability approaches: repair, internal transparency, global transparency, and flexibility**" (A&B 1996, p.70). 회수 출처: Wouters & Groen (2019), *Maandblad voor Accountancy en Bedrijfseconomie*, 각주 3 (OA). **인용자가 Wouters 본인**이라 신뢰도가 높다.
> "**The characteristics of procedures, whether they are enabling or coercive, depend directly on their features**" (A&B 1996, pp.69–70). 회수 출처: Wouters & Groen (2019), 각주 2.
> enabling 유형은 직원이 "**deal more effectively with its inevitable contingencies**" 하도록, coercive 유형은 "**force reluctant compliance and to extract recalcitrant effort**" 하도록 설계된다 (A&B 1996, p.69). 회수 출처: Meiers (2015), PhD diss., The Ohio State University (OhioLINK `osu1428684701`).
> ⭐ 시스템은 "**the features and the uses; the features themselves are influenced by the design process and the goals that govern it**" 을 보고 판정된다 (A&B 1996, p.70). 회수 출처: Mamat (2012), Univ. of Warwick WRAP `wrap.warwick.ac.uk/49474`. ← **이 책 논지에 가장 직접적으로 꽂히는 문장.**
> "**formalization's attitudinal outcomes depend both on the fit of the degree of formalization with the 'routineness' of the task, as argued by contingency theory, and on the type of formalization**" (A&B 1996, p.77). 회수 출처: Meiers (2015). → 4분면(organic / autocratic / **enabling bureaucracy** / mechanistic) 도출의 근거 문장.
> 향후 과제로 "**we need to develop empirical tests of the hypothesized antecedents and consequences of enabling and coercive designs**" (A&B 1996, p.85). 회수 출처: Wouters & Groen (2019), 각주 2.

**네 특성의 정의 — 독립적 2차 정식화 5종 (원문 대조 실패, 아래는 모두 인용자의 서술)**

| 특성 | Souza & Beuren 2018 (Table 3, 조작적 정의; Van Der Hauwaert & Bruggeman 계열) | Ghent 박사논문 (2014, biblio.ugent.be/5719000) | Mamat 2012 (Warwick) | UvA 논문 (2019) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Repair** | "Possibility for the users themselves to repair a process instead to forcing the interruption of the work when faced with a flaw or impasse." | "repair generates procedures that facilitate responses to real work contingencies. Breakdowns and repairs identify problems with formal procedures, which become opportunities for improvement." | "Repair relates to the extent to which employees are able to respond to emerging contingencies and to seek improvements." | "Repair refers to whether non-expert employees are allowed to fix breakdowns caused by system failures or user mistakes." |
| **Internal transparency** | "The logic behind the functioning of a process and the information about its status are available and are presented in an intelligible way." | "users need both an understanding of the logic of the equipment's internal functioning and information on the equipment's status." | "the visibility of the work processes to the employees and … the extent to which they understood the rationale for the rules and procedures." | "the degree to which users are provided with the logic underlying the processes and rules and what constitutes best practice." |
| **Global transparency** | "Availability of information for the user regarding the state of the entire production process." | "technologies that are programmed to provide operators with extensive information relating to the status of the broader production process." | "the visibility of the overall working context in which employees perform their task and the extent to which they understand how a particular operational task relates to the overall system." | "the extent to which users understand how their work fits into the processes of the organization as a whole." |
| **Flexibility** | "Possibility for employees to modify processes to attend to specific demands." | "machines are programmed to give advice and make suggestions, but it is the responsibility of the user to make controlling decisions after the system displays the requisite data." | "the choices of modes of control system that enable the employees to modify certain tasks accordingly." | "the discretion users have to modify their work." |

> 네덜란드어 실무 해설(Wouters & Groen 2019)의 정식화 — 저자가 이 계보의 당사자:
> - **Interne transparantie**: 직원이 지표가 *어떻게 정의되고 계산되는지*를 이해해서 *자기가 무엇을 하면 그 지표가 움직이는지* 안다.
> - **Brede transparantie (global transparency)**: 성과측정 시스템이 *다른 관리통제들과 어떻게 맞물리는지, 조직 전략목표와 어떻게 연결되는지, 내 과업과 어떤 관계인지*를 이해한다.
> - **Flexibiliteit**: "de toegestane mogelijkheid dat medewerkers van voorgeschreven procedures kunnen afwijken" — *"직원이 규정된 절차로부터 벗어나는 것이 허용되는 가능성."*

**한계·반박** **1996년 개념/리뷰 논문이며 자체 대규모 실증 검증이 없다.** 저자들 본인이 p.85에서 실증적 검정의 필요를 적었다(2차 경유). 책에서 "실증적으로 입증된 인과"라고 쓰면 안 되고 **"이후 20년의 실증 연구가 붙은 프레임"**이라고 써야 정확하다. **인용문이 OCR 산출물이다** — 명백한 OCR 오류 관찰(`ISQ9000`→ISO 9000, `TOM`→TQM 등). 원본 대조 필요. **enabling/coercive는 이분법으로 제시되지만 현장은 그렇지 않다** — Ahrens & Chapman(2004)이 두 방식의 **겹침·공존**을 보인다. **이 논문은 "형식화를 더 하라"는 논문이 아니다** — 4분면 중 "낮은 형식화 + enabling = organic"이 따로 있고 과업 정형성과 형식화 정도의 적합(fit)이 전제된다. **AX처럼 비정형성이 높은 영역에 두꺼운 표준을 깔면 유형이 enabling이어도 정도 불일치로 부작용이 난다** — 이 단서를 빼면 오독이다. Adler의 NUMMI/토요타 낙관 해석에 대한 비판(Berggren 등과의 논쟁)이 실재한다.

## 5-2. Ahrens & Chapman (2004) — 회계·관리통제로의 첫 이식

**서지 [PR]** Thomas Ahrens & Christopher S. Chapman, "Accounting for Flexibility and Efficiency: A Field Study of Management Control Systems in a Restaurant Chain," *Contemporary Accounting Research* **21(2): 271–301**, **2004년 6월**(온라인 2004-05-27). **DOI `10.1506/vjr6-rp75-7gux-xh0x`** (Crossref 확인). OA 아님. 초록 전문은 **University of Bristol 리포지토리**에서 회수.

**⭐⭐ 초록 전문 (VERBATIM) — 네 원리가 여기 그대로 나열된다**
> "While some field studies have suggested that management control systems can be used simultaneously to make organizations more efficient and more flexible, the contingency literature has found it difficult to address this issue in the absence of a clear and comprehensive typology for analyzing more processual uses of management control systems. This paper distinguishes between enabling and coercive uses of management control systems. **Coercive use refers to the stereotypical top-down control approach that emphasizes centralization and preplanning. In contrast, enabling use seeks to put employees in a position to deal directly with the inevitable contingencies in their work. The design principles that underlie the enabling use of management control systems are repair, internal transparency, global transparency, and flexibility.** Through a detailed analysis of a single-case field study carried out over a two-year period, we illustrate how management pursued the objectives of efficiency and flexibility by using management control systems in enabling ways. We suggest that the four design principles of enabling use can facilitate field studies of management control systems, but that they can also be used to define an enabling typology for contingency researchers to analyze the ways in which organizations simultaneously pursue efficiency and flexibility through their management control systems."

*(**coercive한 사용은 중앙집권화와 사전계획을 강조하는 전형적인 탑다운 통제 방식을 말한다. 반대로 enabling한 사용은, 직원이 자기 일에서 불가피하게 마주치는 우발 상황을 직접 다룰 수 있는 위치에 서게 하려 한다. enabling한 사용의 밑에 깔린 설계 원리는 repair, internal transparency, global transparency, flexibility다.**)*

**추가 인용 (2차 문헌 경유 — Mamat 2012, Warwick WRAP)**
> ⭐ "**attempts to mobilize local knowledge and experience in support of central objectives**" (Ahrens & Chapman 2004, **p.296**) — enabling한 MCS 사용의 정의

*(현장의 지식과 경험을, **중앙의 목표를 뒷받침하는 방향으로** 동원하려는 시도.)*

→ **p.296의 이 한 줄이 당신 테제의 학술적 원문에 가장 가깝다.** "bottom-up creates the seeds, top-down harvests them" ≒ "mobilize local knowledge and experience **in support of central objectives**". 중앙 목표를 버리는 것이 아니라 **중앙 목표를 현장 지식으로 채우는** 구조다.

> "**top down control approach that emphasizes centralization and pre-planning**" (Ahrens & Chapman 2004, **p.271**) — coercive한 사용

**이 책에 쓸 수 있는 부분**
1. **효율 vs 유연성이 트레이드오프가 아니라는 실증 사례.** "거버넌스를 세우면 속도가 죽는다"는 반론에 대한 직접 대응.
2. **이분법 깨기가 실무적으로 가장 쓸모 있다.** 같은 조직이 어떤 통제는 coercive하게(식품안전·보안·개인정보), 어떤 통제는 enabling하게(실험·프롬프트·툴 선택) 운영해야 한다. **"전부 열거나 전부 잠그거나"가 아니라 영역별 배치**라는 설계 지침.

**한계·반박** **단일 사례 현장연구, N=1** — 해석적 사례이지 인과 추론이 아니다. **수직통합된 레스토랑 체인** — 과업이 상대적으로 정형적·물리적이며, 지식노동·AI 맥락으로의 직행 이전은 저자들이 보증하지 않는다. **"사용(use)"과 "설계(design)"의 혼선** — A&B는 시스템의 *features*를 말했는데 A&C는 *uses*로 옮긴다. **"표준을 enabling하게 만들었다"와 "관리자가 그것을 enabling하게 쓴다"는 다른 문제**임을 분리해야 한다. ⚠️ **네 원리를 레스토랑 체인의 어떤 통제 실무에 어떻게 조작화했는지(본문 세부)는 전문 미확보로 확인하지 못했다.**

## 5-3. Wouters & Wilderom (2008) — "무엇을"이 아니라 "어떻게 만들었나"

**서지 [PR]** Marc Wouters & Celeste Wilderom, "Developing performance-measurement systems as enabling formalization: A longitudinal field study of a logistics department," *Accounting, Organizations and Society* **33(4–5): 488–516**, **2008년 5월**(온라인 선공개 2007-06-22). **DOI `10.1016/j.aos.2007.05.002`** (Crossref 확인). 피인용 413회(S2). OA 아님(Twente 리포지토리 PDF 링크 404 직접 확인). 초록은 University of Twente 연구정보 페이지에서 회수.

**초록 전문 (VERBATIM)**
> "This paper reports on a developmental approach to performance-measurement systems (PMS). In particular, **we look at characteristics of a development process that result in the PMS being perceived by employees as enabling of their work, rather than as primarily a control device for use by senior management.** We will refer to such a PMS as 'enabling PMS'. The theoretical part of the study builds on ideas of enabling versus coercive formalization [Adler, P. S., & Borys, B. (1996). Two types of bureaucracy: Enabling and coercive. Administrative Science Quarterly 41 (March), 61–89]; on notions of organizational learning (e.g., [Zollo, M., & Winter, S. G. (2002)…]); and on awareness of the incompleteness of performance measures (e.g., [Chapman, C. S. (1997)…; Lillis, A. M. (2002)…]). **The empirical context entails a mixed-method, 3-year longitudinal study of the logistics department of a medium-sized company in the beverage manufacturing industry.** Qualitative data were gathered through interviews, participation in meetings, action research, and review of company documents. **We also analyzed two waves of quantitative survey data, gathered from a panel of 42 employees. We find that a development process that is experience-based contributes to the enabling nature of the PMS, as it builds on existing skills, local practices, and know-how on performance measurement to enrich the PMS step-by-step over time. Also, experimentation with specific performance measures was found to enhance the enabling nature of the PMS: testing, reviewing, and refinement of conceptualizations, definitions, data, and presentations of new performance measures. Professionalism was significantly related to positive attitude toward performance measures in our survey data. The results also illustrate that transparency of the PMS itself is key to enabling PMS.**"

> ⚠️ **초록에는 A&B의 네 특성을 인용한 문장이 없다.** 각주 형태의 A&B 서지 인용만 있다.

**⭐ 이 책에 쓸 수 있는 부분**
1. **한 줄 요지: "무엇을 만드느냐"가 아니라 "어떻게 만드느냐"가 enabling을 결정한다.** 중앙이 완성된 표준을 던지면 coercive가 되고, **현장의 기존 노하우 위에 단계적으로 얹으면** enabling이 된다. → **중앙이 만드는 것은 "완성품"이 아니라 "누적 경로"다.**
2. **experimentation이 시스템 개발의 정식 단계로 등장한다.** "표준을 정하고 배포"가 아니라 "프로토타입 지표를 돌려 보고 정의를 다듬는 사이클".
3. **"transparency of the PMS itself is key"** — 대시보드가 무슨 숫자를 보여 주느냐보다 **그 숫자가 어떻게 계산되는지가 보이느냐**가 관건.

**한계·반박** **N=1 부서, 42명 패널, 2회 측정** — 정량 부분의 검정력이 낮고, **저자들이 개입한 액션 리서치라 관찰자 효과가 결과와 분리되지 않는다.** 음료 제조업 물류 — 반복적·계측 가능한 영역. ⚠️ **"professionalism was significantly related to positive attitude"는 상관이며, 계수·유의수준·표본 세부는 전문 미확보로 확인하지 못했다.** 대안 설명: enabling으로 지각되는 이유가 "개발 과정"이 아니라 단순히 **참여 자체의 심리적 소유감**일 수 있다.

## 5-4. Wouters & Roijmans (2011) — 프로토타입이 지식통합 장치다

**서지 [PR]** Marc Wouters & Diana Roijmans, "Using Prototypes to Induce Experimentation and Knowledge Integration in the Development of Enabling Accounting Information," *Contemporary Accounting Research* **28(2): 708–736**, **2011**(온라인 2010-11-25). **DOI `10.1111/j.1911-3846.2010.01055.x`** (Crossref 확인).
**오픈액세스 워킹페이퍼 판본 [WP]:** SSRN, **DOI `10.2139/ssrn.1601211`** (2010). 본문 PDF는 Cloudflare 차단으로 미확보, **초록 전문은 Crossref의 SSRN DOI 레코드에서 VERBATIM 회수.**

**초록 전문 (VERBATIM, SSRN WP 판본)**
> "We investigate enabling performance measurement systems (PMS) in operations, i.e., systems that are perceived by employees as assisting with their daily work. We contrast enabling PMS with those that are primarily a control device by senior management. **Previous research in accounting suggests that the development of enabling PMS requires room for experimentation by users, as well as cross-functional knowledge integration. How this can be done has received less attention.** Building on the new product development literature, we investigate how prototypes can support experimentation and knowledge integration in the context of accounting. The empirical results are based on a longitudinal case study, using action research. We study the process of developing a performance measurement system for transportation costs. **In this process, employees from various functions and departments had to cooperate as no single employee possessed sufficient knowledge about operations, accounting and information systems by oneself. The process consisted of several cycles of experimentation in which prototypes of the new accounting system were used as integrating devices. The prototypes were based on traditional accounting tools, such as variance analysis, and on information provided by the employees during the discussions. This process gave the employees many new insights and led to a new accounting system that was accepted by all of them.** We identify several characteristics of effective experimentation and knowledge integration in accounting."

**⭐ 이 책에 쓸 수 있는 부분**
1. **"아무도 혼자서는 충분한 지식을 갖고 있지 않았다"** — AX 시스템 설계의 조건 그대로다. 도메인 지식은 현장에, 데이터·인프라 지식은 플랫폼팀에, 규제·재무 지식은 스태프에 흩어져 있다. 그래서 **중앙이 혼자 표준을 못 만든다.** 도덕적 권고가 아니라 **지식 분포에서 도출되는 구조적 제약**이다.
2. **"프로토타입 = 통합 장치(integrating device)"** — "중앙이 공급하는 것은 표준·자원·제도"라고 할 때, **그 제도의 구체적 형태 하나가 "돌려볼 수 있는 프로토타입"**임을 보여 준다. 문서화된 가이드라인이 아니라 **작동하는 초안**이 지식을 끌어모은다.
3. **"a new accounting system that was accepted by all of them"** — 수용은 설득의 결과가 아니라 **공동 제작의 부산물**이다.

**한계·반박** **액션 리서치 단일 사례** — 연구자가 개입해 만든 성공 사례이며 실패 사례와의 비교가 없다. **"모두가 수용했다"는 결과 변수가 자기보고·해석적**이며 수용의 지속성 증거는 초록 수준에서 확인되지 않는다. ⚠️ **본문에서 A&B 네 특성을 어떻게 조작화했는지, "효과적 실험과 지식통합의 몇 가지 특성"이 구체적으로 무엇인지는 확인하지 못했다.**

Therefore, it cannot be affirmed that a PMS with clear information on goals, objectives, vision, and mission, and that is transparent with respect to the causal relationships between these elements and the actions of employees, both locally and in the overall structure of the company, or that enables problems to be identified and solved in an active way, **can lead to better task performance.**"

**⭐⭐ 책에서의 쓸모 — 논지를 정교화하는 지점** enabling한 시스템은 **성과에 직접 꽂히지 않는다**(β=0.0101, p=0.4761 — 사실상 0). 오직 **심리적 임파워먼트를 경유해서만** 성과로 간다(간접효과 0.2626, p=0.0064). → **"좋은 표준을 깔았는데 왜 성과가 안 오르나"에 대한 답이 여기 있다.** 표준은 사람이 스스로 결정하고 영향력을 행사한다고 느끼게 만들 때만 작동한다.

**한계** 단일 조직·단일 시점 횡단 설문(N=88), 자기보고 성과. PLS-SEM은 인과를 확립하지 않는다. competence 차원은 R² adj가 음수(−0.033). **VIF가 2.7~3.7이고 repair↔flexibility 상관이 0.703** — **네 특성이 실증적으로 잘 분리되지 않는다는 신호이며, A&B 프레임에 대한 정당한 반박 근거다.** ⚠️ Table 7의 Value 컬럼이 표준화 계수인지 여부는 명시 미확인.

## 5-7. Zittrain — 생성성(generativity) 정의와 요인

> ⚠️ **의뢰문의 "Zittrain 2006 다섯 요인"은 오류다.** 2006년 HLR 논문에는 **네 요인**만 있고(본문 p.1982 "As defined by these **four** criteria"), **transferability는 2008년 단행본에서 추가**되었다.

### (a) Zittrain (2006), "The Generative Internet"

**서지 [PR]** (법학 리뷰 — 학생 편집 저널이므로 엄밀한 익명 심사는 아님. 이 단서를 남긴다.)
Jonathan L. Zittrain, "The Generative Internet," *Harvard Law Review* **119(7): 1974–2040** (2006). 무료 전문: **Harvard DASH, handle `1/9385626`** — DASH REST API로 원본 PDF 직접 다운로드·전문 판독 완료.

**생성성의 정의 (VERBATIM, p.1980)**
> "**Generativity denotes a technology's overall capacity to produce unprompted change driven by large, varied, and uncoordinated audiences.** The grid of PCs connected by the Internet has developed in such a way that it is consummately generative."

*(**생성성이란, 크고 다양하며 서로 조율되지 않은 청중에 의해 추동되는, 요청받지 않은 변화를 만들어 내는 어떤 기술의 총체적 역량을 가리킨다.**)*

**네 요인 — 총괄 (VERBATIM, p.1981)**
> "**Generativity is a function of a technology's capacity for leverage across a range of tasks, adaptability to a range of different tasks, ease of mastery, and accessibility.**"

**개별 정의 (VERBATIM, pp.1981–1982)**
> **1. Capacity for Leverage:** "…**leverage describes the extent to which these objects enable valuable accomplishments that otherwise would be either impossible or not worth the effort to achieve.** … **A generative technology makes difficult jobs easier. The more effort a device or technology saves — compare a sharp knife to a dull one — the more generative it is. The greater the variety of accomplishments it enables — compare a sharp Swiss Army knife to a sharp regular knife — the more generative it is.**"

> **2. Adaptability:** "**Adaptability refers to both the breadth of a technology's use without change and the readiness with which it might be modified to broaden its range of uses.** … **A technology that offers hundreds of different additional kinds of uses is more adaptable and, all else equal, more generative than a technology that offers fewer kinds of uses. Adaptability in a tool better permits leverage for previously unforeseen purposes.**"

> **3. Ease of Mastery:** "**A technology's ease of mastery reflects how easy it is for broad audiences both to adopt and to adapt it: how much skill is necessary to make use of its leverage for tasks they care about, regardless of whether the technology was designed with those tasks in mind.** … **Ease of mastery also refers to the ease with which people might deploy and adapt a given technology without necessarily mastering all possible uses.** … **That is, much of the pencil's generativity stems from how useful it is both to the neophyte and to the master.**"

> **4. Accessibility:** "**The more readily people can come to use and control a technology, along with what information might be required to master it, the more accessible the technology is. Barriers to accessibility can include the sheer expense of producing (and therefore consuming) the technology, taxes and regulations imposed on its adoption or use (for example, to serve a government interest directly or to help private parties monopolize the technology through intellectual property law), and the use of secrecy and obfuscation by its producers to maintain scarcity without necessarily relying upon an affirmative intellectual property interest.**"

> **5. Generativity Revisited (p.1982):** "**As defined by these four criteria, generativity increases with the ability of users to generate new, valuable uses that are easy to distribute and are in turn sources of further innovation.** It is difficult to identify in 2006 a technology bundle more generative than the PC and the Internet to which it attaches."

**이 책에 쓸 수 있는 부분** **"unprompted change"(요청받지 않은 변화)가 핵심어다.** "bottom-up creates the seeds"는 곧 unprompted change이고, 네(다섯) 요인은 **"어떤 인프라라야 요청받지 않은 변화가 나오는가"에 대한 설계 명세서** — 중앙이 공급해야 할 것의 목록이다. **접근성 장벽 세 가지가 그대로 대기업 AX의 실패 원인 목록이다** — 라이선스 비용, 승인·보안 규제, 그리고 **"생산자가 희소성 유지를 위해 쓰는 비밀주의"**(= 플랫폼팀이 문서를 공개하지 않는 것).

**한계·반박** 법학 논문이며 실증 연구가 아니다. 2006년 시점의 진단임을 논문 자신이 못박는다("in 2006"). **가장 강한 반박은 Zittrain 자신에게서 나온다** — 생성성은 **취약성**이기도 하다. "열면 좋다"가 아니라 **"열면 남용도 온다, 그래서 통제 요구가 생기고, 그 통제가 생성성을 죽인다"**는 딜레마가 이 논문의 진짜 주제다. **생성성을 낙관적으로만 인용하면 원저 왜곡이다.**

### (b) Zittrain (2008), *The Future of the Internet — And How to Stop It*

**서지 [BOOK]** Jonathan Zittrain, *The Future of the Internet — And How to Stop It*, New Haven & London: **Yale University Press, 2008**. With a New Foreword by Lawrence Lessig and a New Preface by the Author. **ISBN 978-0-300-15124-4 (pbk.)**; LCCN **2008942463**. 라이선스: "The author has made an online version of this work available under a **Creative Commons Attribution-Noncommercial-Share Alike 3.0 License.**" 무료 전문 PDF(다운로드·전문 판독 완료): `https://futureoftheinternet.org/wp-content/uploads/sites/12/2013/06/ZittrainTheFutureoftheInternet.pdf` (Harvard DASH handle `1/4455262`에도 동일본)

**생성성의 정의 (VERBATIM, p.70)**
> "I have termed this quality of the Internet and of traditional PC architecture 'generativity.' **Generativity is a system's capacity to produce unanticipated change through unfiltered contributions from broad and varied audiences.** Terms like 'openness' and 'free' and 'commons' evoke elements of it, but they do not fully capture its meaning, and they sometimes obscure it.
> **Generativity pairs an input consisting of unfiltered contributions from diverse people and groups, who may or may not be working in concert, with the output of unanticipated change.** For the inputs, how much the system facilitates audience contribution is a function of both technological design and social behavior. **A system's generativity describes not only its objective characteristics, but also the ways the system relates to its users and the ways users relate to one another. In turn, these relationships reflect how much the users identify as contributors or participants, rather than as mere consumers.**"

> ⚠️ **2006년판과 2008년판의 정의는 다르다.** 2006: "**unprompted** change driven by large, varied, and **uncoordinated audiences**" / 2008: "**unanticipated** change through **unfiltered contributions** from broad and varied audiences."

**다섯 요인 — 총괄 (VERBATIM, p.71, "FEATURES OF A GENERATIVE SYSTEM")**
> "What makes something generative? **There are five principal factors at work: (1) how extensively a system or technology leverages a set of possible tasks; (2) how well it can be adapted to a range of tasks; (3) how easily new contributors can master it; (4) how accessible it is to those ready and able to build on it; and (5) how transferable any changes are to others—including (and perhaps especially) nonexperts.**"

**개별 정의 (VERBATIM, pp.71–73)**
> **Leverage (p.71):** "**Leverage makes a difficult job easier.** Leverage is not exclusively a feature of generative systems; non-generative, specialized technologies can provide leverage for their designated tasks. **But as a baseline, the more a system can do, the more capable it is of producing change.** … **A little effort can thus produce a very powerful computer program**, whether a file-sharing program or a virus comprising just a few lines of code."

> **Adaptability (pp.71–72):** "**Adaptability refers to how easily the system can be built on or modified to broaden its range of uses.** A given instrumentality may be highly leveraging yet suited only to a limited range of applications. For example, TiVo is greatly leveraging—television viewers describe its impact on their lives as revolutionary—but it is not very adaptable. … **A technology that affords hundreds of different, additional kinds of uses beyond its essential application is more adaptable and, all else being equal, more generative than a technology that offers fewer kinds of uses. The emphasis here is on uses not anticipated at the time the technology was developed.** A thick Swiss Army knife may have plenty of built-in tools compared with a simple pocket knife, but many of those are highly specialized."
> → **맥가이버칼(내장 도구가 많음)과 진짜 적응성은 다르다.** 사내 AI 플랫폼에 기능을 100개 넣는 것과 생성성은 별개다.

> **Ease of mastery (p.72):** "**A technology's ease of mastery reflects how easy it is for broad audiences to understand how to adopt and adapt it.** The airplane is not readily mastered… Paper, on the other hand, is readily mastered… **Ease of mastery also refers to the ease with which various types of people might deploy and adapt a given technology, even if their skills fall short of full mastery.** A pencil is easily mastered: it takes a moment to understand and put to many uses, even though it might require a lifetime of practice and innate artistic talent to achieve Da Vincian levels of leverage from it. **The more useful a technology is both to the neophyte and to the expert, the more generative it is.** PCs and network technologies are not easy for everyone to master, yet many people are able to learn how to code, often (or especially) without formal training."

> **Accessibility (pp.72–73):** "**The easier it is to obtain access to a technology, along with the tools and information necessary to achieve mastery of it, the more generative it is. Barriers to accessibility can include the sheer expense of producing (and therefore consuming) the technology, taxes, regulations associated with its adoption or use, and the secrecy its producers adopt to maintain scarcity or control.** … **Standard PCs are very accessible; they come in a wide range of prices, and in a few keystrokes or mouse-clicks one can be ready to write new code for them. On the other hand, specialized PC modes—like those found in 'kiosk mode' at a store cycling through slides—cannot have their given task interrupted or changed, and they are not accessible.**"

> ⭐⭐ **Transferability (p.73):** "**Transferability indicates how easily changes in the technology can be conveyed to others. With fully transferable technology, the fruits of skilled users' adaptations can be easily conveyed to less-skilled others.** The PC and the Internet together possess very strong transferability: a program written in one place can be shared with, and replicated by, tens of millions of other machines in a matter of moments. **By contrast, a new appliance made out of a 75-in-1 Electronic Project Kit is not easily transferable because the modifier's changes cannot be easily conveyed to another kit. Achieving the same result requires manually wiring a new kit to look like the old one, which makes the project kit less generative.**"

→ **이 요인이 당신 테제의 "수확(harvest)"에 가장 정확히 대응한다.** 현장이 만든 것이 다른 현장으로 자동 복제되지 않으면, 씨앗은 났는데 수확 시스템이 없는 것이다. **그리고 von Hippel의 확산율 표(5.0~21.2%)가 바로 이 transferability의 실패를 계측한 값이다.**

**상호작용과 임계 (VERBATIM, p.74)**
> "…**the five qualities of leverage, adaptability, ease of mastery, accessibility, and transferability often reinforce one another. And the absence of one of these factors may prevent a technology from being generative.** A system that is accessible but difficult to master may still be generative if a small but varied group of skilled users make their work available to less-sophisticated users. **Usually, however, a major deficiency in any one factor greatly reduces overall generativity.**"

→ **AX 플랫폼 진단 프레임: 다섯 축 중 가장 낮은 축이 전체를 결정한다.**

**⭐⭐ 미루기 원칙 (procrastination principle) — p.31 (VERBATIM)**
> "**The procrastination principle rests on the assumption that most problems confronting a network can be solved later or by others. It says that the network should not be designed to do anything that can be taken care of by its users.** Its origins can be found in a 1984 paper by Internet architects David Clark, David Reed, and Jerry Saltzer. In it they coined the notion of an 'end-to-end argument'… **The end-to-end argument stands for modularity in network design**… More generally, **the procrastination principle is an invitation to others to overcome the network's shortcomings, and to continue adding to its uses.**"

→ **이 절 전체에서 당신 테제에 가장 잘 맞는 한 문장일 수 있다.** 중앙이 미리 다 풀지 않는 것이 **게으름이 아니라 설계 원칙**이고, 그 미완성이 곧 **초대장**이다.

**생성적 패턴 6단계 (VERBATIM, p.99)**
> "1. An idea originates in a backwater. / 2. It is ambitious but incomplete. It is partially implemented and released anyway, embracing the ethos of the procrastination principle. / 3. Contribution is welcomed from all corners, resulting in an influx of usage. / 4. Success is achieved beyond any expectation, and a higher profile draws even more usage. / 5. Success is cut short: 'There goes the neighborhood' as newer users are not conversant with the idea of experimentation and contribution, and other users are prepared to exploit the openness of the system to undesirable ends. / 6. There is movement toward enclosure to prevent the problems that arise from the system's very popularity."

→ **사내 AX 도구의 수명 주기를 그대로 예언한다.** 6단계(울타리)에서 무엇을 하느냐가 리더의 진짜 시험대다.

> **생성성의 역설 (p.100):** "**The paradox of generativity is that with an openness to unanticipated change, we can end up in bad—and non-generative—waters.**"

**한계·반박** 단행본이며 실증 연구가 아니다(규범적·역사서술적 논변). **저자가 예견한 "tethered appliance"로의 이동은 실제로 일어났고(앱스토어·모바일·클라우드), 그럼에도 그 위에서 대규모 혁신이 계속 일어났다** — **Zittrain의 비관 예측 자체가 부분적으로 반증되었다**는 것이 표준적 반론이다. **"unfiltered contributions"는 기업 조직에서 문자 그대로 불가능하다** — 보안·개인정보·규제가 필터를 강제한다. 조직 내부로 옮길 때는 "무필터"가 아니라 **"필터의 비용을 낮추는 것"**으로 번역해야 하며, **이 번역을 저자가 승인한 바는 없다.**
